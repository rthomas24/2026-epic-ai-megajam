# Triad Drift art direction

Working reference for ART-01, November 13. Use the existing ship colours and silhouettes to identify the squad in motion, and the same colours on weapons and pilot cards. Review hull detail on foot in the hangar and combat readability at chase distance. Nicholas and Ryan review the look in game. | Subject | Recognition cues |
|---|---|
| Rift · Wren | Crescent wings, an open portal ring at the nose, violet hull and magenta emission `#FF2BD6`. At 3–4 m the rim of the ring, cockpit frame and wing edges should remain distinct. [Selected three-view sheet](../../ArtSource/EngAI/Ships/Rift/concept_r2/source/sheet-Rift-g26_PICK.png) |
| Lancer · Kato | Long needle fuselage, forward-swept wings, ice white and cyan `#3FE9FF`. Keep the outline readable while the camera follows the ship. |
| Bastion · Moss | Wide hull, paired missile pods and dome emitter. Amber `#FFB21E` identifies the heavy ship in the squad. |
| Null Armada and Dominion | Angular black hulls, crimson seams `#C3122E` and a white eye `#F4F7FF`. Enemy shots read red or orange. Check the eye, engine glow and outline against the actual sky at 300 m and 1 km. The Dominion's three flank bands and illuminated docking mouth identify it on approach. |

## Light and colour by location

| Location | What the player needs to see |
|---|---|
| Galaxy, menu and victory | The sun and nebula define scale and rim light. Keep fighters readable across bright and dark sky. The live menu and victory backdrops should leave enough contrast for white text, cyan accents and keyboard or controller focus. |
| Verdance | Teal foliage, violet ridges and cyan bioluminescence. Keep the landing site, relay and buried-structure scan legible as the view moves from orbit to ground. |
| Cinder | Dark basalt and ash frame amber magma and cyan crystals. Distinguish the anchor's charge ports from the hot terrain both from the bridge and at interaction distance. |
| Dominion interior | Black metal and gunmetal with red strips and white lenses. Keep the pilot's route, consoles and reactor threats readable through alarm and collapse states. |

## Director's Cut

Open each wave or objective with its scene and take slate. Ship switches use a cut, a brief letterbox and the pilot name. A retake uses the CUT card and updates the take number. In the opening cinematic, use the specified 2.39:1 framing and shot exposure. During play, preserve the existing card clock, input and readable text. The proposed short underline reveal has a static reduced-motion version awaiting gameplay review.
