# Triad Drift

A missile is on your tail. Switch to Rift and send it through a portal. Cut to Lancer, line up the enemy that fired it, and ram straight through. When the next wave closes in, take Bastion and throw a shield over your wingmen. The two ships you leave behind keep fighting.

Fly between two worlds to give your squad new abilities. On Verdance, search a wreck in the glowing jungle and wake an ancient relay. On Cinder, fight through the outpost and silence its jammer. Then bring all three ships to the Dominion, break its shields and strike the exposed reactor.

Every pilot switch is a camera cut. A clean ram or a missile turned back on its owner can earn a money shot. Lose the take and the director calls for another.

## Play on Windows

Extract the full Windows build to a folder and launch the game executable inside. Choose **New Game** to start the first scene. Use **Settings** to adjust controls and sound.
