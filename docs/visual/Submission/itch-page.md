# Triad Drift

A missile is on your tail. Switch to Rift and send it through a portal. Cut to Lancer, line up the enemy that fired it, and ram straight through. When the next wave closes in, take Bastion and throw a shield over your wingmen. The two ships you leave behind keep fighting.

Fly between two worlds to give your squad new abilities. On Verdance, search a wreck in the glowing jungle and wake an ancient relay. On Cinder, follow the survey records to an excavation, recover the crystals and disable the field anchor. Finish Cinder and clear the fifth wave to open the approach to the Dominion. Its four shield generators stand between your squad and the ship.

Every pilot switch is a camera cut. A clean ram or a missile turned back on its owner can earn a money shot. Lose the take and the director calls for another.

## Play on Windows

Extract the full Windows build to a folder and launch the game executable inside. Choose **New Game** to start the first scene. Once you have control, press **1** for Rift, **2** for Lancer or **3** for Bastion. On a gamepad, press left or right on the D-pad to cycle through the squad. Use **Settings** to adjust controls and sound.
