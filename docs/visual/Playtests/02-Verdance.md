# Verdance — device, transfer defence and extraction

Start from a named save after the tutorial withdrawal. Use ordinary flight and landing inputs. Budget 12–18 minutes including one retry; record actual time. Record build, pilot, input device, resolution and save point in the ENG-46 log.

1. Follow the Verdance bearing. Time one hyperdrive jump; record where it ends relative to the target. Descend, land and dismount. Capture the space-to-ground transition and first footstep. Check the horizon, landing silhouette, terrain collision and any exposed loading frame. Listen for the Verdance theme after the reveal and whether it competes with combat music.
2. Recover the signal-analysis device before using the survey console. Verify Bastion earns Gravity Well once and the HUD identifies the award. Record the device and reward together. No Cloak award is due here.
3. Activate the terminal. Capture the buried-structure graphic both on the console and in the scan display. Check that the scan, blue optic and captions stay readable in gameplay lighting, and the music ducks so the fragmented voices and three separated pulses are audible. Follow C3_Kato_Artificial through C3_Wren_HoldThem. Record missing or placeholder sound separately from correct subtitle text.
4. Defend the transfer for 60–90 seconds across three waves. Observe the meter while an enemy reaches the terminal and after it is cleared; note the third-wave Heavy. Complete the transfer and verify Rift earns Cloak here. The approved path uses transfer defence for Cloak; report any remaining required relay climb.
5. Die during the defence on a second attempt. Check checkpoint, meter, unlock state and number of reward announcements after the retake. Quit after a confirmed save, Continue, and verify the device and earned abilities persist without replaying the investigation. Extract and take off. Listen for C4_Command_Proceed and the Verdance theme fading.

Record the tested build and timestamps for arrival, both unlocks, terminal ducking and takeoff in docs/eng/PLAYTEST-*.md. A debug stage may isolate a failure; label it separately from the connected journey.
