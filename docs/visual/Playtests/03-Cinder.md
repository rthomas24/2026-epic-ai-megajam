Start at Verdance departure with Gravity Well and Cloak earned. Allow 15–25 minutes including a retake. Log build, input, pilot and resolution. Keep game audio and HUD visible during the following checks.

1. Play waves 3–4 on the Cinder route. Check the interception objective, the enemy anticipation clue, ship switching and power feedback. Compare ship silhouettes against debris at combat speed. Distinguish a failed cue from one masked by music or weapons.
2. Descend and dismount. Follow the patrol route to the survey station. Inspect floor contact and visibility on the dig rim. At the terminal, compare the solid amber Cinder scan with the dashed cyan Verdance overlay. Check the single Kato echo, the Origin Core naming and tentative temporal-device interpretation. 3. Secure the excavation through the gate, combat areas and Warden. Take the crystal cache and EMP prototype. Capture the HUD and A3_CrystalsFound. Lancer receives EMP Pulse once; listen for the prototype explanation. Verify the map still directs the player to the anchor.

Inspect objective text and the marker immediately after shutdown; the Dominion shield and four generators are separate. Check one retake on this route for restored safe footing, correct charge count and no second EMP award.
5. At extraction, save and Continue. Verify cache and anchor flags persist. Take off; capture the C6 transmissions and the change to Break the Dominion guard. While wave 5 is uncleared, check that the passage remains closed. Record current Cinder music or silence without assuming a final cue has been delivered.
