# Dominion assault — four generators and docking

Use a save after Cinder with wave 5 still active. Allow 10–15 minutes for a full attempt and a saved restart. Record build, flown ship, input and audio settings.

1. Before clearing wave 5, inspect the approach barrier and objective. Clear the wave and approach with Cinder complete; record the moment the field opens. On a separate save with Cinder incomplete, use an available wave-five checkpoint to check the other half of the gate. If no such save exists, mark that branch untested.
2. Listen for Push The Attack at assault entry. Keep fighting across one full track return. Record entry, return and final handoff against gameplay events; listen for a second ordinary combat cue underneath. An audio file in the package alone cannot pass this check.
3. Destroy one generator at a time. Start at 0/4, make a short recording of each count and marker disappearing. Check G6_Kato_MarkingTargets, G6_Kato_ShieldWeakening and G6_Command_ShieldCollapsed. The shield drops on the fourth generator. Listen for G6_Clue1 and G6_Clue2; the speaker remains unidentified. Inspect generator highlights and turret fire for readable contrast against the hull.
4. Die during an assault and retake. Save at an available checkpoint, quit and Continue. Record the restored generator count and compare it with the saved state. Check that destroyed generators, their markers and the music do not restart independently.
5. Dock through the opened bay. Check tractor assist, the two wingmen holding outside and the handoff to the pilot of the ship actually landed. Record where assault music ends and boarding music begins. Repeat with a different pilot if a checkpoint permits; name untested pilots. Check the bay mouth for clipping and the landed craft for clear team identification.

Submit the objective and audio timestamps, count progression, tested pilot(s), registered build and actual elapsed time to docs/eng/PLAYTEST-*.md.
