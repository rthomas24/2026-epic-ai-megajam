# Reactor — defenders, conduits and heart

Use a save just before the sealed reactor threshold. Budget 10–15 minutes, including one failure and Continue. Record pilot, build, difficulty, resolution and input.

1. Play the recovered transmission to completion. On another run, try Skip before ten seconds, then at or after ten seconds. Verify safe movement near the sealed entrance and the same essential reveal and open-door state on full playback and eligible skip. Capture the live Wren order if played.
2. Enter separately as Wren, Kato and Moss. Record all three future pilots in each run. Confirm the two active defenders are Kato/Moss when Wren boards, Wren/Moss when Kato boards and Wren/Kato when Moss boards. Verify the player meets their own counterpart. Record actual dialogue and attack readability. Flag a missing counterpart, a third active defender or a drone fallback separately.
3. Strike the four conduits using normal blade damage. Check that the active targets and 0/4 count match the objects the player sees. Record the sweeping laser’s telegraph, damage and a readable way to evade it. A perfect deflect is optional. Watch for overlapping effects obscuring either the blade or the laser.
4. Try the heart before the fourth conduit, then break the last conduit and strike the exposed heart. Capture the HUD transition and heart destruction once. Log which encounter music is heard and whether lines or danger cues are masked; the reactor music selection may still be provisional.
5. Die before breaking the heart and retake. Quit after an available checkpoint and Continue; compare conduit count, defender state and objectives against the saved checkpoint. After a successful kill, keep recording through A5_Escape and measure the handoff to the escape hit (target 2.5 seconds). Confirm the escape starts once.

Post timestamps, exact build, encounter variant, actual time and any reproducible failures in docs/eng/PLAYTEST-*.md.
