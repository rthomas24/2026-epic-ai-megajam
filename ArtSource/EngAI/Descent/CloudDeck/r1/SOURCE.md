## Pack
- The pick was chosen over seed 72727 for more even lighting (easier to tile
  and to tint) and cleaner black gaps for alpha. Raw wrap MSE of the pick was
  about 48 horizontally and 709 vertically (not tileable as generated).
- A 96 px cosine wrap-blend with a half-period roll was applied on X and Y,
  then a 1 px exact seal so opposite edges match (wrap MSE = 0). A faint soft
  blend can still be seen at the wrap in a 2×2 if you look for it.
- Alpha = `clip(((luma - 8) * 1.08 / 255) ^ 0.95 * 255)` where luma is
  `0.299R+0.587G+0.114B`. Mean alpha is about 183.
