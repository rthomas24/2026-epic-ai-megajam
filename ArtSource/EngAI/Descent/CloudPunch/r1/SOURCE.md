## Pack
- ffmpeg extracted all 150 frames (960×960) from the source MP4.
- Mean luma is already 0.421 on frame 0 (the clip opens on a full cloud wall),
  peaks at frame 88 (mean luma 0.611) and falls to 0.0036 on frame 149.
  The full 0–149 range was subsampled to 64 frames. No crossfade (one-shot).
- Each cell is resized to 256×256 (LANCZOS). Alpha = `clip((luma - 4) * 1.15)`
  where luma is `0.299R+0.587G+0.114B`.
