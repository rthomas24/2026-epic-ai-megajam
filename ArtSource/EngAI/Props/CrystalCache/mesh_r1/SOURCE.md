## Blender polish (4.5.3 LTS)

Each asset ships an editable `.blend`:

|---|---|---|

| `SM_*` delivery mesh, **live modifiers** | planar-dissolved (12°) Rodin cage, sharp from angle, **Bevel** 0.6 cm / 1 segment / angle 70° / harden normals + **WeightedNormal** (keep sharp, 50, face area) | **Bevel** 0.25 cm / 1 segment / angle 30° / harden normals (`hardsurface.add_bevel`) + **WeightedNormal** (`hardsurface.add_weighted_normal`) |

| Socket | `SOCKET_Crystal` | — |

`rebuild.py` re-exports either FBX from its `.blend` through the repo's `export_for_unreal.py` (modifiers applied on a temporary copy, `SRC_*` skipped, FBX `UnitScaleFactor` **100**, checked in both files). Running it reproduces the committed FBX exactly (50,376 / 1,384 tris).

Bevel segments are 1 on purpose: 2 segments pushed the crystal's 1–25° "wobble" edges from 3.7% to 57.6% and the cradle to 62,862 tris with 129 non-manifold edges; 1 segment is a crisp chamfer that catches light without the soft look.

Fixes in this pass: `SOCKET_Crystal` had been left at the cradle origin, moved to the cradle floor; the earlier cradle FBX carried the mesh name `SM_CrystalCacheCradle.001` (its `UCX_` would not have matched in Unreal), re-exported from the editable mesh; stale `.001` material names cleaned; crystal given its `SRC_` and live modifiers; per-asset `.blend` files split out of the assembly; AO re-baked per mesh in isolation (the first bake had the crystal inside the cage, so its AO was black).

## Gameplay C++ check (main 27d97402) — mismatches

- **No code references `SM_CrystalCache*` yet.** The cache interaction is `TDPlanetCourse.cpp` Cinder outpost objective step 5: an `ATDInteractableActor` with `ConsoleMesh = SM_Cind_PrototypeConsole` (**160 × 160 × 140 cm**, comment "the scale below"), spawned at `(XEnd − 700, Y0 + 700, 70 cm)`, yaw 180°, `SetActorScale3D(1.6, 1.6, 1.4)`, prompt "Take the crystals and the EMP prototype", `HoldTime` 1.5 s.
- To use this cache there: **actor scale 1.0** (the 1.6/1.6/1.4 scale would make the cradle 320 × 576 × 252 cm) and **Z = ground**, not 70 cm (our pivot is at the base; the 70 cm suits the centred placeholder cube). The footprint grows from 160 × 160 to **200 × 360 cm**, so the spot 700 cm off the arena line needs ~180 cm clearance along the long axis; yaw 180° turns the emblem toward −X.
- Interaction traces hit the mesh's collision on the `Interactable` channel, so the cradle's UCX box works as the hold-E target; the crystal mesh can sit on `SOCKET_Crystal` and be hidden on claim.
- The story beat is "crystals **and** the EMP prototype" in one interaction. The reference board (and so this cache) has **no EMP prototype object**; keep `SM_Cind_PrototypeConsole` beside the cache, or ask for a small EMP-device prop for the cradle floor.

## Textures (2K)

Authored in Blender / numpy from the Rodin PBR set (cradle) and from baked vertex attributes (crystal). Not byte-identical to `source/`.

- **BC** — cradle: linear-space lift ×1.28 (emissive strips protected by the E mask), covered-texel median sRGB **59.8 → 67.5**; then the 49% of empty UV space (the octagon rings pack hollow) was filled by push-pull dilation, so mips and the whole-image audit no longer read black. Whole-image median **62 (p50; <20: 0%)**. Crystal: unchanged colours, empty space dilated, median **78**.
- **N** — tangent-space Cycles bake of a Bevel node (cradle r 6 mm, crystal r 1.8 mm) plus subtle micro-bump, green flipped for Unreal DirectX.
- **ORM** — R = AO re-baked per mesh **in isolation** in Cycles (AO distance 0.30 m cradle / 0.12 m crystal, 96 samples, 16 px margin), covered-texel median **0.54** (cradle) / **0.62** (crystal; crystal faces buried inside neighbouring shards bake black and are never seen), empty UV space dilated; G = roughness; B = metal.
- **E** — cradle orange strips (thresholded Rodin emissive); crystal cyan/amber inner glow.

## Import notes

- FBX, uniform scale 1.0, combine meshes, Import Normals, no lightmap UV generation, collision from `UCX_`.
- `_BC`, `_E`: sRGB on. `_N`: Normalmap, already DirectX, do **not** flip green. `_ORM`: sRGB off, Masks (R = AO, G = rough, B = metal).
- Nanite fine for the cradle (opaque, 50K). Keep the crystal non-Nanite if its material goes translucent.
