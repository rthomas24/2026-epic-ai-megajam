**Geometry.** Each crystal is a hex prism with a pyramid tip, the `procedural_rocks.py` `_crystal` idea re-implemented as in r1. Every facet is planar. The apex is offset by up to ~0.3 of the radius, and the tip faces stay planar triangles. Every shaft-to-tip crease measures at least 25° (most are 30–50°), so it reads, and counts, as a hard edge. Two extra coplanar ring stations, at 14% and 40% of the shaft, carry the colour ramp; they add only 0° (flat) edges. Each root is buried in the nest but never goes below the nest's flat underside.

- **Footprint:** assembled bbox X -46.0..42.7 cm, Y -67.5..73.4 cm. That is inside the cage interior: about ±85 cm in X at mid-height, and the octagon end rings start at |y| ≈ 120 cm.

The nest's flat underside sits on the floor plate at 30.05 cm, so a few low rock faces (between 33 and 39 cm) bed into the ribs (3 triangle pairs). This is intended: the rubble sits in the grating. | Object | |
|---|---|

| `SM_CrystalCacheCrystal` (**live modifiers**) | **Bevel** 0.25 cm / **1 segment** / angle 52° / harden normals (`hardsurface.add_bevel`), then **WeightedNormal** (keep sharp, 50, face area; `hardsurface.add_weighted_normal`). Material `M_CrystalCacheCrystal`. |
| `UCX_SM_CrystalCacheCrystal_00..14` | The convex hulls, parented to the mesh. Collinear ring points are removed, so no hull has a zero-area sliver. |

The bevel angle is 52°, up from r1's 30°. It chamfers the 60° prism edges and the 90° root caps. It leaves the 25–50° tip creases as single crisp edges: chamfering those split each one into two ~20° "wobble" edges, which raised wobble to 29% in a test. `rebuild.py` re-exports the FBX from the `.blend` through the repo's `export_for_unreal.py`, giving the same 3,574 tris and FBX `UnitScaleFactor` **100** (checked).

## Textures (2K, baked from per-corner colour attributes on the un-bevelled mesh; UVs carry through the live Bevel)

- **BC**: the facet colours. Each face of each shard gets its own deep-teal-to-cyan value, the tips go pale and icy, and the roots are amber-tinted. The basalt is dark ash. Covered-texel median sRGB lum is ~156; the cyan is deliberately lighter than r1's (78) so the crystals read as glassy rather than dark teal. The empty UV space is filled from the nearest covered texel (EDT dilation), so mips stay clean.
- **N**: a tangent-space Cycles bake of a Bevel node (r 1.8 mm) with a faint micro-bump, then **green flipped for Unreal (DirectX)**.
- **ORM**: R = Cycles AO (0.12 m, 96 samples; covered median 0.72, floored at 0.08 for faces buried inside other shards), G = roughness (0.08–0.13 crystal, ~0.8 basalt), B = metal (0.02 crystal, 0.06 basalt).
- **E**: cyan inner glow that fades toward the tip, the amber root ramp, the amber crystals at full strength, and amber spill on the nest. The Blender preview uses emission strength 2.2, as in r1.
