## Why this is an AI mesh (R-043)

This is a **distant, non-walkable landmark** (seen at 100+ m; the player plants charges at the plinth and runs). Under R-043 it stays a Rodin mesh with a Blender cleanup and texture pass. It is not a walkable interior and should not be used as one.

## Blender polish (4.5.3 LTS)

Each asset ships an editable `.blend` in `mesh_r1/`:

- `SRC_CinderAnchorTower` / `SRC_CinderAnchorEmitter` — the cleaned, scaled raw Rodin mesh, **hidden**, no modifiers.
- `SM_*` — delivery mesh at real scale in metres (FBX UnitScaleFactor 100 → Unreal cm). Sharp edges from `set_sharp_from_angle(30°)`, shade smooth, and a live **`WeightedNormal`** modifier (`keep_sharp=True`, `weight=50`, `mode=FACE_AREA`) left **unapplied**.
- `UCX_*` and `SOCKET_*` are in the file.
- `rebuild.py` exports the FBX with modifiers applied.

**Bevel vs smooth+WN test** (audit B3a, logged in `stats.json`): a light Bevel (angle 50°, width 0.4% of height, 2 segments) plus Weighted Normal produced **no triangle change** — Cycles/Blender clamped the overlap on this dense 50K triangulation, so the bevel contributed nothing. Route chosen: **`smooth_by_angle(30°) + WeightedNormal`**, which preserves the original UVs for the Rodin bake and does not open holes. Edge highlights come from the **baked bevel + micro-bump normal** (B3b), not from a geometry bevel.

Non-manifold / boundary edges were fixed to **0 / 0** on both visual meshes (the held `/tmp/cinder-anchor-r1/` staging had 6 and 3 after a COLLAPSE decimate of the emitter). The emitter is **not decimated** (49,976 tris, under the 150K Nanite cap).

The tower keeps four hanging-cable islands (plus the main body = 5 welded islands). Two micro-fragments (≤ 20 faces) were removed from the tower; the emitter had none of consequence.

## Scale, pivot, sockets

Assembled height is **40.00 m** (tower 35.782 m + emitter 5.972 m seated in the top well, 1 cm radial clearance).

- Pivot at the ground-base centre (`min Z = 0.0 cm`, centred in X and Y). +Z up, **+X forward** (a charge port faces +X after a −0.60° yaw).
- Prefer the FBX (`UnitScaleFactor` 100, Unreal scale 1.0). The GLB is clean glTF **in metres**, +Z up, ground at Z = 0 (accessors: tower `[−7.10, −7.13, 0] … [7.10, 7.13, 35.78]`). Blender's glTF importer remaps to Y-up on reimport, so a naive Blender reimport bbox looks swapped; the file itself is correct. Use the FBX for Unreal.

### Charge sockets vs C++ (important)

`SOCKET_Charge_0..2` sit at radius **590.1 / 601.3 / 587.1 cm**, z ≈ 270.9 / 259.1 / 275.4 cm, on the −X-side plinth faces, headings 133.6° / 179.6° / -136.2° (C++ headings 128.4° / 180.0° / 231.6°).

C++ currently plants the three charges on a **520 cm** ring at z = 60 cm (`TDPlanetCourse.cpp`, `Cos(A) * 520, Sin(A) * 520, 60`). The tower's base plinth is **r ≈ 752 cm**, so that ring is buried inside the plinth. **Use the sockets**, not the 520 cm ring.

`SOCKET_TopEmitter` is at (0, 0, 3402.8 cm) — the floor of the top well (lip inner r = 212.2 cm, depth = 175.4 cm, collar outer r = 516.9 cm).

## Textures (2K)

All maps were authored in Blender / numpy from the Rodin PBR set. Not byte-identical to `source/`.

- **BC** — linear-space lift (audit B3c / `lift_albedo.py`), emissive ports and glow rings protected by the E mask so they stay vivid and unclipped. Gunmetal target sRGB median 60–80.
  - Tower: p5/p25/p50/p75/p95 = 25 / 47 / **66** / 88 / 135 (was 5 / 15 / **24** / 35 / 57). `<40` fell from 83% to 16.9%.
  - Emitter: p5/p25/p50/p75/p95 = 21 / 43 / **63** / 91 / 161 (was 10 / 27 / **40** / 63 / 132).
- **ORM** — R = baked Ambient Occlusion (Cycles; emitter used an AO node at 0.45 m so local cavities read without crushing the interior). G = roughness remapped to **0.32–0.58** (median ≈ 0.47 / 0.42). B = metallic **0.55–0.80 on armour plates**, **0.05–0.20 on dark trim / cables** (distribution from luma + source metal).
- **N** — tangent-space bake in Cycles of a Bevel node plus a subtle object-space micro-bump, then green flipped (`255−G`) for Unreal DirectX. Flat texels (`<2°`) dropped from **91.1% → 70.4%** (tower) and **81% → 52.9%** (emitter). Tower: 17.4% at 6–24°, 3.4% `>24°`. Emitter: 24.1% at 6–24°, 13.5% `>24°`.
- **E** — thresholded Rodin emissive (thr = 16), 1 px halo, unboosted.
