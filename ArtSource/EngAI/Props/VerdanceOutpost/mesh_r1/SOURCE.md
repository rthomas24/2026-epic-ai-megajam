## Raw source inspection

Inspected on 2026-11-13 with Blender 4.5.3 (headless). The files have no unit
metadata; glTF's default unit is the metre.

### Outpost (`source/outpost/base_basic_pbr.glb`)

| | |
|---|---|
| Tris / verts | 8,058 / 11,167 (all triangles; 5,195 unique verts in the raw FBX) |
| Extents (after Blender glTF import, Z-up) | 1.832 × 1.902 × 1.574 units (X × Y × Z) |
| Up axis | **+Z** (the raw mesh was centred on the origin, not sitting on Z = 0) |
| Open front / ramp | raw **−Y** (lowest mid-height density; ramp tip at the −Y extreme, near the ground) |
| Pivot | AABB centre, not the ground-base centre |
| Textures | 2K PNG: diffuse, normal (OpenGL / glTF Y+), packed metallic-roughness (`texture_pbr.png`), plus loose roughness and metallic. **No emissive and no ambient-occlusion map.** |

`source/outpost/texture_pbr.png` is already packed as R = 255 (white AO), G = roughness, B = metallic, and is
byte-identical to the delivery `T_VerdanceOutpost_ORM.png`. The diffuse has no isolated light islands that
need an emissive mask (bright pixels are the yellow hazard stripes).

## Derived delivery (what to import)

Reoriented, pivoted and scaled in Blender 4.5.3. Source files were not modified.
The outpost was rotated +90° about Z so the open bay and ramp face **+X**, then
uniformly scaled so the longest horizontal (ramp tip to back of the deck) is
**800.0 cm**, then dropped so the deck legs sit on Z = 0 and the origin is the
ground-base centre. The console was yawed so the emissive screens and controls
face **+X**, uniformly scaled so overall height is **150.0 cm** (width lands at
**260.0 cm**), and given the same ground-base origin. No decimate was applied.

| Uniform scale | 1.366862 from the raw import (applied in metres, then exported) |

**Desk-height note.** The 85.8 cm control surface is below the 100–110 cm
standing-desk brief because of the mesh proportions (the desk sits at about
57% of overall height in both the concept and the Rodin mesh). Height was
set to 150.0 cm so width lands at 260.0 cm — the two bay-fit targets. Scaling
the desk onto 105 cm would push overall height to 191 cm and width to 331 cm,
past those targets and a tighter fit in the 338 cm bay. The silhouette stays
faithful to `refs/42_console_clean.png`.

**FBX (`SM_VerdanceOutpost.fbx`, `SM_ResearchConsole.fbx`) — preferred for Unreal.**
Exported with the repo's Unreal convention (`apply_unit_scale=True`,
`apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_UNITS'`, `axis_forward='-Z'`, `axis_up='Y'`).
Both files carry `UnitScaleFactor` **100** in the raw FBX bytes (verified
against `SM_EnergyBlade_Deployed.fbx` and `SM_NullBomber.fbx`). Re-imported in
Blender 4.5.3 at the centimetre sizes above, with min Z ≈ 0 and the origin at
the ground-base centre. Uniform scale in Unreal should be 1.0.

**GLB** is a secondary, centimetre-scale, Z-up companion (`export_yup=False`,
mesh scaled ×100 before export). Prefer the FBX for Unreal.

## Delivery textures

All maps are 2048×2048 PNG.

| Map | Layout |
|---|---|
| `T_VerdanceOutpost_BC.png` / `T_ResearchConsole_BC.png` | sRGB albedo, copied from Rodin `texture_diffuse.png` |
| `T_VerdanceOutpost_N.png` / `T_ResearchConsole_N.png` | DirectX normal. Rodin / glTF shipped OpenGL (Y+); the green channel was flipped (`255 − G`) for Unreal. Leave Flip Green **off** on import. |
| `T_VerdanceOutpost_ORM.png` / `T_ResearchConsole_ORM.png` | R = AO (white; Rodin shipped no AO), G = roughness, B = metallic. Byte-identical to the source `texture_pbr.png`. Masks, sRGB off. |
| `T_ResearchConsole_E.png` | sRGB emissive mask. A luminance threshold of 12 was applied to the raw `texture_emissive.png` to drop residual near-black noise (~0.1% of pixels). The cyan waveform screen, status / button lights and cyan specimen-chamber glow are preserved (3.97% of pixels remain on). There is no outpost emissive. |

## Import notes for the builders

- Unreal: prefer the FBX at uniform scale 1.0. BC default / sRGB on; N as Normalmap,
  **DirectX** (Flip Green off); ORM as Masks, sRGB off (R AO, G roughness, B metallic);
  console E as emissive, sRGB on. The game can tint the console mask.
- First check, outpost: 800.0 cm ramp-to-back, 770.4 cm wide, 662.0 cm tall, legs
  on Z = 0, ramp and open bay toward +X, doorway 282.9 cm high at X = +230 cm,
  bay hollow.
- First check, console: 260.0 cm wide, 150.0 cm tall, 87.0 cm deep, screens toward
  +X, origin on the ground at the centre. Place on the outpost deck (Z = 138.0 cm)
  at the back of the bay, facing the ramp, with walk-up space in front; the
  contact sheet's composite row shows this placement with a 180 cm pilot box.
- Combined 14,686 tris, well under 25k. No collision or sockets in this drop.
