**Scale note (R-042).** Nicholas's reference board footprints the cabin at **9.6 m** with a 1.8 m human. At that uniform scale Rodin built a correctly hollow, furniture-free bay with a flat floor, but the interior soffit only cleared **164.0 cm** (the outer door lip was taller; a dropped ceiling just inside the facade is what a walking pilot hits). R-042 requires interior ceilings **≥ 280 cm** and doorways **≥ 140 × 240 cm**. Uniform scale ×1.737507 from the 9.6 m mesh sets interior clear height to **285.0 cm** (safe margin above 280) and the doorway to **625.5 × 312.1 cm**. The overall footprint (**16.68 × 15.55 m**, 12.32 m to the antenna tips) is larger than the 8–13 m guideline because walkable interior height is the hard requirement. The 11.0 m drill rig was left at the briefed mast height. A 180 × 80 cm pilot box stands on the interior floor in the doorway and in the bay (and in the Y = 0 cutaway) with about 105 cm of headroom under the 285 cm soffit.

**FBX (`SM_CinderDrillRig.fbx`, `SM_CinderSiteCabin.fbx`) — preferred for Unreal.**
Exported with the repo's Unreal convention (`apply_unit_scale=True`,
`apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_UNITS'`, `axis_forward='-Z'`, `axis_up='Y'`).
Both files carry `UnitScaleFactor` **100** in the raw FBX bytes. Re-imported in
Blender 4.5.3 at the centimetre sizes above, with min Z ≈ 0 and the origin at
the ground-base centre. Uniform scale in Unreal should be 1.0.

**GLB** is a secondary, metre-scale, Z-up companion (`export_yup=False`,
`export_apply=True`, PBR materials wired). Prefer the FBX for Unreal. See the
import note if an importer assumes glTF Y-up.

## Import notes for the builders

- Unreal: prefer the FBX at uniform scale 1.0. BC default / sRGB on; N as Normalmap, **DirectX** (Flip Green off); ORM as Masks, sRGB off (R AO, G roughness, B metallic); E as emissive, sRGB on.
- First check, rig: 781.5 × 413.8 × 1,100.0 cm, tracks on Z = 0, mast and cab toward +X, cab roof 311.1 cm, 22,000 tris.
- First check, cabin: 1,668.0 × 1,554.9 × 1,232.2 cm, posts on Z = 0, stairs and open bay toward +X, doorway 625.5 × 312.1 cm, interior clear 285.0 cm, hollow bay, 22,000 tris.
- Combined 44,000 tris. No collision or sockets in this drop. The cabin's front access is stepped stairs at 28.2°, not a 20° ramp.
