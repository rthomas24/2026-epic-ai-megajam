## Prompts (verbatim)

System prompt, used on all three mine jobs:

```
Generate original cinematic concept art for the indie game Triad Drift. Original designs only. Never add text, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, UI, or existing copyrighted intellectual property.
```

### Hero — spiked core, red arming light (seed 432001)

```
Cinematic 16:9 product-concept hero, no text, no labels. An original space plasma mine: a dark spiked core about 2.8 metres across, gloss-black armour #0E0E12 with crimson seams #C3122E, irregular hard-surface spikes and faceted plates, not a round naval contact mine and not a cartoon bomb. A single bright red arming light, hot #FF1E3C with a near-white centre, sits in a recessed socket like a focused eye; two smaller red pips may accompany it. The arming light is the readability cue and throws a faint red glow on the nearby spikes. The mine floats in dark space with colourful nebula rim light. Photoreal, original design, no logos.
```

### Trail — bomber, four mines, Rift for scale (seed 432002)

```
Cinematic 16:9 space shot, no text, no labels. A dark angular Null bomber about 11.8 metres long, gloss black with crimson seams, is laying a trail of four plasma mines. Each mine is about 2.8 metres across: a dark spiked core and a bright red arming light. The mines are clearly smaller than the bomber. In the mid-ground, a 5-metre violet crescent-wing fighter with a circular portal ring at the nose and magenta engine glow gives a second scale reference. Colourful nebula, photoreal, original ship and mine designs, no existing franchise likeness.
```

### Cinematic 300 m readability (mood) (seed 432003)

```
Cinematic 16:9 combat-distance readability test, no text, no labels, no HUD. The camera is about 300 metres from a sparse trail of four dark spiked plasma mines in deep space. At this range the mine bodies are only small dark silhouettes, but each has a sharp, readable red arming light — a bright point that still pops against the colourful nebula and dark sky. A faint 11.8-metre bomber silhouette is even farther along the trail. The mines must feel genuinely far away, not like close-up props shrunk in frame. Photoreal, original design.
```
