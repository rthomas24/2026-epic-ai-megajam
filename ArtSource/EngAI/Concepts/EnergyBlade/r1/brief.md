# Energy blade r1 — brief

Reference-only concept sheet for the pilot energy beam remake (issue #163). The Niagara implementation is owned by the blade-effects builder; this folder is art direction, not a shipped asset.

## Lock

- **Handle is Direction B** (approved, mesh r1). White ceramic, dark wrap, knuckle spine. Not redesigned.
- Deployed: barrel Ø 3.70 cm, pommel-to-guard 24.6 cm, tip-to-pommel 39.7 cm, extents 24.9 × 4.2 × 35.9 cm.
- Folded: 12.0 × 4.5 × 23.7 cm.
- Energy is a VFX edge on the rail: hot near-white core, coloured glow, soft bloom, flicker. A thin single-edged wedge / ribbon, **not** a cylindrical lightsaber tube and **not** a baked volume.
- A 180 cm figure is required on this human-scale sheet.
