## Notes on first takes

- Colour and ignite product shots are the **authority for beam proportion**: energy is a thin ribbon on the Direction B rail, a short one-handed tool (39.7 cm tip-to-pommel).
- The hangar key is for the required 180 cm figure and bloom in context. The beam may read longer than the rail in that frame.
- The swing panel is effect-led (ribbon trail, sparks shed on the arc, hit spark). It is more illustrated than the product shots, and the hardware in that frame is looser; use colours and ignite as the hilt authority.
- Cross-section is an illustrated callout: wedge / crescent, not a tube. Hardware still comes from the product shots.
- Original design only — no lightsaber likeness was requested, and the ignite is a crawl along the rail, not a lengthening cylinder.
