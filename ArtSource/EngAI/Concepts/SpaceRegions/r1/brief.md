# Space regions r1 — mood and silhouette reference

Reference-only concept sheets for three space regions from
`docs/eng/SPACE-DESIGN.md` (R-042 ask 3). These are art-direction boards, not
shipped assets and not meshes.

| Sheet | Region | Silhouette from 5 km | Its piece of the story |
|---|---|---|---|
| `sheet_ShepherdBelt.png` | The Shepherd Belt | A bright band across the sky from anywhere in the system | The system's old belt: the reason this was a patrol station. Calm, ordered, Keplerian. |
| `sheet_QuietGraveyard.png` | The Quiet Graveyard | A line of dark hulls lit by one blinking beacon | The research expedition that went quiet: the place the signal device was taken from. Still, cold, no rocks. |
| `sheet_SurveyScar.png` | The Survey Scar | Fractured metal and violet crystal rocks, rig lights | Null survey rigs have mined these rocks hollow and haul the yield to Cinder. |

Each board is a labelled layout: a key mood image, a silhouette and scale view
with the player ship (Rift, 5.00 m — the approved mesh) for scale, measured
scale bars from the design doc, and a sRGB palette strip. A 180 cm figure is
omitted; at region scale (80–400 m hulls and moonlets) it would not read.

Instanced set pieces (moonlets, research hulls, survey rigs, hollowed rocks)
stay on hold until the Lead posts that brief (R-042 item 5).
