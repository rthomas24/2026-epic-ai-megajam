```
Generate original cinematic concept art for the indie game Triad Drift. Original designs only. Never add text, labels, captions, logos, watermarks, UI, or existing copyrighted intellectual property.
```

### Key — from behind the ship, looking toward the bay (seed 433001)

```
Cinematic 16:9 concept art, no text, no labels. The camera sits just behind a 5-metre violet crescent-wing fighter with a circular portal ring at the nose and magenta engine glow, looking forward toward a huge dark Dominion mothership. The docking bay mouth on the brow is open. A 9-metre-diameter cyan-glow tractor emitter sits in the mouth. A volumetric cyan and pale-white tractor-beam CONE comes out of the bay toward the camera and the ship: from this viewpoint the cone narrows ahead toward the emitter, with a half-angle of about 35 degrees, wrapping softly around the fighter. The ship is inside the cone and is being pulled in. Soft volumetric light, cyan glow, Dominion gloss-black hull with crimson seams. A wide physical cone of light, not a dotted laser and not a single thin beam. Original designs, photoreal colourful sci-fi, huge scale.
```

### Establishing — cone volume (seed 433002)

```
Cinematic 16:9 establishing shot, no text, no labels, slightly behind and to the side of a 5-metre violet crescent-wing fighter so both the ship and the full tractor cone are visible. Ahead, a huge dark Dominion mothership with an open docking bay. A volumetric cyan tractor-beam cone originates at a 9-metre emitter in the bay mouth and opens outward at a 35-degree half-angle, reaching a long way toward the camera (the gameplay cone is 3 kilometres deep). The fighter is a small craft inside that volume. Soft cyan volumetric light, gloss-black hull, crimson seams, colourful nebula. Wide physical cone, not a thin laser. Photoreal, original designs, huge scale.
```
