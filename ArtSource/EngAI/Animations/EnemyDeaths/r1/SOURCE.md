## Root motion
**With root motion.** Every clip has its horizontal travel on the `root` bone (smoothed pelvis XY, Z = 0, no rotation), as
in PR #86's deaths. With root motion off in Unreal the body still travels with the root (exactly like the raw motion); with
it on, the capsule follows. Travel was reduced in Blender (column "travel" below) so it stays at or under about 1 m:
Grunt 77-86 cm, Heavy 86-96 cm (x1.08 in game = 93-104 cm), Warden 99-101 cm (a 260 cm body falling flat cannot end much
closer than its own hip height). The code holds the last frame (`PlayAnimation(..., false)`), so all clips end still and on the floor.

"Lowest mesh point" is the lowest vertex of the skinned in-game mesh over every frame: nothing goes more than **1.2 cm**
below the floor at any frame; once down, the body rests on its lowest point (0.0-2.6 cm above / -1.2 cm below while it
settles). "Foot slide while standing" counts horizontal ankle travel while the pelvis is above 70 % of its standing height
and the ball of the foot is on the floor (re-imported FBX); the remaining 3-5 cm on three clips is the pivot of the
planted foot as the body twists into the fall. "Still from" = the frame after which no joint moves faster than 3 cm/s.

## Weight per character
- **Grunt** (light brawler, fists raised, closed fists): played at the generated speed, 2.57 s, falls fast.
- **Heavy** (shield bearer): played at **x0.90** (3.27 s), prompts ask for a slow topple "like a toppling wall".
- **Warden** (260 cm boss): played at **x0.85** (3.93 s) on top of a 3.4 s generation; a body 1.62x the size takes about
  1.27x longer to fall under the same gravity, so the slower read is physical, not floaty.

## Clean-up done in Blender (`scripts/retarget.py`, per-clip settings in `scripts/configs.json`)
1. **Retarget** SMPL-H to the in-game skeleton by world-rotation transfer with a per-bone rest alignment (PR #86's method),
   sampled at the character's playback speed (sub-frame sampling).
2. **Travel** reduced by scaling the pelvis' horizontal displacement (per clip, below).
3. **Foot plants and clean steps:** foot contacts while standing are pinned with two-bone leg IK (median plant position);
   between two plants, and from the last plant to the moment the body leaves its feet, the foot moves on a lifted arc
   (7 cm, x character scale) instead of skimming the floor.
4. **No kicks at the end:** from the start of the fall the legs (and for some clips the pelvis / spine, "core") ease into
   the final lying pose, optionally part-way toward straight legs, so they fold instead of flailing; the pose is then held
   (8-frame ease) until the end of the clip. Warden_Back also damps the chest and arms in the first second (the raw arch
   flung the chest shield over its head).
5. **Warden side falls:** the Warden's shield is rigid on its chest (`spine_05`, see `ArtSource/EngCharacters/Warden/SOURCE.md`), so lying on a side
   leaves the slab standing up like a fin, or props the body. Death_Left / Death_Right therefore **roll 50° onto the
   back** after landing (frames below).
6. **Floor contact:** each frame the body is raised or lowered so the mesh's lowest point is on Z = 0 once the body is down,
   and never more than 1.2 cm below the floor at any frame (two passes, before and after the foot IK).
7. Root motion extracted to the `root` bone (9-frame smoothing); export from frame 1, armature only.

## Honest caveats (please check these in the capture)
- **Nobody has watched these at full speed in the game, and Unreal was not started.** Previews are Blender workbench
  renders of the in-game meshes (`contact_sheet.jpg`, `previews/`).
- **Fall direction is diagonal on three clips:** Grunt_Back falls forward and to its right (about 49° off straight ahead),
  Heavy_Front falls back and to its left (about 40°), Warden_Back forward-right (about 38°). They still read as "shot from
  behind / the front", but they are not straight.
- **Warden lying pose:** Warden_Back lies face down on its chest shield, so the pelvis rests 70 cm high (the slab props
  it); Warden_Front rests 72 cm high for the same reason (gun arm and shield under / beside it). Death_Left and
  Death_Right end on the back after a 50° roll, not on the side, because of the rigid chest shield. If the shield ever moves
  to `hand_l`, the side falls could be re-cut from the same raw clips without the roll.
- **Root motion is on** (77-101 cm), like PR #86. If the code keeps root motion off, the mesh still ends where the root
  ends; the capsule stays at the start (collision is disabled on death in `HandleDeath`, so that only matters for the
  corpse's bounds).
- Travel was cut by x0.45-0.85, so falls cover less ground than the raw motion: the stagger steps are shorter and the body
  "lands" closer to the feet. This can look slightly compressed on the longest raw falls (Heavy_Left x0.45, Warden_Front x0.45).
- Limbs ease into the end pose during the fall (to stop the generator's leg kicks); the body is a little stiffer in the air
  than the raw motion.
- Fingers are a fixed relaxed curl (Grunt: closed fists), not animated; twist bones are not driven (as PR #86).
- The Heavy's shield is part of the `SK_NullHeavy` skinned mesh; nothing drops or detaches.
- No foot IK correction for slopes: the clips assume flat ground.

## Rebuild
`blender -b --factory-startup --python scripts/retarget.py -- source/<raw>.fbx out/<name>.fbx '<json config>'` with the
character block (`_chars`) merged into the clip's block from `scripts/configs.json`, plus `rootmotion: true, grip: 0.6,
vthresh: 3.0` (what `build.py` adds). Then `render.py` on the saved `.blend`, `compose.py`, `verify.py`.
