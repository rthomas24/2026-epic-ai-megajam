## Skeleton, units, root
- Bone names and hierarchy are exactly the in-game 88 bones from `ArtSource/EngCharacters/Crew/export/` (`SK_NullTrooper.fbx`, `SK_NullGrunt.fbx`, `SK_NullHeavy.fbx`): UE5 Manny names, armature object `root`. Twist bones stay at rest relative to their parents (not driven). `ik_hand_gun` / `ik_hand_r` follow `hand_r`, `ik_hand_l` follows `hand_l`, `ik_foot_l/r` follow the feet.
- **Units:** FBX 7.4, `UnitScaleFactor` **100**, Y-up, 30 fps (`TimeMode` 6 / `CustomFrameRate` 30), the same as #86 and #91. (Later attack clips in #92 used UnitScaleFactor 1.0; both import to the same size in Unreal, which converts units.)
- **Root motion:** only the Heavy stagger has travel on the `root` bone (smoothed, Z = 0, no rotation). The five loops and the Grunt combo have a static root. With root motion off in Unreal the staggering Heavy still travels with the root, exactly like the raw motion; with it on, the capsule follows.
- Scale: motion is mapped by leg length (×1.098: SMPL-H ~170 cm to the 185 cm crew body).

## Clean-up done in Blender (`scripts/retarget.py`, per-clip settings in `scripts/configs.json`)
1. **Retarget** SMPL-H to `SKEL_NullCrew`: each mapped bone copies the source bone's world rotation change from its rest pose, after aligning the target's A-pose bone directions to the source's T-pose directions.
2. **Floor contact:** each frame the body is lowered or raised so the lowest point of the character mesh approaches Z = 0 (with a small clamp; see caveats).
3. **No foot sliding on locked contacts:** foot contacts are detected (ankle low and slow) and pinned with a two-bone leg IK, blended at each end. Planted slide on those intervals is **0.0 cm** (see `verify.json`).
4. **Closed rifle grips (trooper only):** a virtual rifle rides on `spine_05`; both hands are solved onto it with two-bone arm IK in aim mode for the whole clip, same method as #86's `Idle_Aim`. The red rifle in the contact sheet is a preview only and is not in the FBX.
5. **Loop closure** on the four loops: last frame matches the first (max bone seam 1.8-4.8 cm).
6. **Closed fists** on the Grunt combo.
7. Exported keyed from time 0, mesh included for preview. Reimport checks are in `verify.json` / `metrics.json`.

## Picks (why these takes)
- Trooper strafe left: seed 7101 over 7102 (backward drift, rising pelvis) and 7103 (a 280 cm/s hitch). Clean left travel, rifle up in every sampled frame.
- Trooper strafe right: seed 7201 over 7202 (21 frames with both feet off the ground) and 7203 (travelled left, the wrong way). 7201 has no flight phase.
- Trooper backpedal: seed 7301 over 7302 (faster, 4-frame flight) and 7303 (almost as much sideways as back). Steady 33 cm/s, no flight.
- Grunt combo: seed 7401 over 7402 (turns away mid-combo) and 7403 (the two hits land almost together). 7401 is a clear left hook then right cross, 6 cm of travel, back to the same fighting stance.
- Heavy stagger: seed 7501 over 7502 (mostly sideways, almost a knockdown, does not return to a shield-up pose). 7501 starts and ends in a braced shield guard.
- Heavy shield walk: seed 7602 over 7601 (a run: 33 frames with both feet off the ground). 7602 is a walk, shield held in front.

## Caveats
- **In-place locomotion.** The trooper strafes / backpedal and the Heavy shield walk are in-place loops so the crew AI can play them while the capsule moves in code. They are not root-motion travel cycles.
- **Generated walks.** HY-Motion locomotion can slide; contacts were locked where the detector found a planted foot. Some steps have shorter lock windows than an authored walk (the Heavy stagger in particular has few auto-detected contacts because the feet are moving). Visual review of the sheet should be the gate.
- **Loop hitch.** The four loops have a one-frame hand-speed spike around frame 85 as the loop-closure blend meets the rifle / arm pose. Start and end poses still match (seams above). It is not a rotation snap (none over 40° after wrapping).
- **Floor.** The mesh dips up to about 1.2 cm under Z = 0 on some frames (same order as #86 / #91). Boot soles stay at about 10 cm.
- **No skinned rifle.** The trooper's rifle is a virtual IK prop, as in #86. Builders should keep using the in-game rifle socket / attach.
- **Grunt timing.** The two impacts are at 0.633 s and 0.967 s, slower than the current single-hit contacts (0.45-0.53 s). That is expected for a two-hit combo; T5 will want timers (and maybe a play-rate) to match. The clip was not time-warped, so the hits stay on the model's own snaps.
- **Heavy stagger length** is 1.47 s, longer than the authored 1.00 s stagger. It recovers to a shield-up stand.
