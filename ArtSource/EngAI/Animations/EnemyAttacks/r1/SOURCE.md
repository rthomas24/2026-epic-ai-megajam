## Grunt charge - how it was made and what it measures
HY-Motion wouldn't produce a run without a flight phase: all eight grunt generations either sprinted (3.8-9 m/s with
both feet off the ground) or stooped into a slow shuffle. So the clip is **hybrid**:
- **Upper body** from `gruntD_s4801` (leaning, arms swinging tight): a 24-frame cycle (source frames 36-60)
  resampled to 16 frames, cross-faded so it loops, with an authored **shoulder drop** (20 deg roll, right shoulder
  low) and **15 deg twist** (right shoulder leading); the head is counter-rolled so it stays level-ish.
- **Legs procedural:** left touchdown frame 1, right touchdown frame 9, stance 8.5 frames each, so frames 9 and 17
  are double-support and **one foot is always on the floor** (grounded run / "groucho" gait, no flight phase).
  86 cm stride, 12 cm foot lift, heel peel onto the ball before toe-off, pelvis lowered where needed so the legs
  never fully lock (max 96 % reach). Feet IK-pinned while planted (0.0 cm slide).
- Measured: **torso lean 41 deg mean** (35-46), **left shoulder 9.2 cm higher than the right**, right shoulder
  6.3 cm ahead, loop seam 0.0 cm.

## Cleanup done in Blender (`scripts/retarget.py`, per-clip settings in `scripts/clips.json`)
1. World-space rotation transfer SMPL-H -> target per bone with rest-pose swing alignment; elbows and knees solved
   as hinges with forearm roll spread onto the twist bones.
2. **Retime** with time-warp knots (`clips.json`) so the impacts land on frames 22 / 40.
   Heavy: anticipation stretched, shove kept fast, recovery slowed. Warden: the model's 1.0 s raise kept, the 7-frame
   downswing kept, the bottom hold shortened and the rise sped up to fit the 0.6 s `RecoverLeft`.
3. Yaw correction so each clip faces -Y; `root` kept still (in place).
4. **Feet:** plant windows (Heavy: left steps forward at frames 15-18, right follows at 19-24) and two-bone leg IK
   to pinned, flat-soled, floor-height targets; 8 cm lifted arcs for the steps. Warden feet force-planted throughout.
5. **Warden only - collision guards for its rigid parts:**
   - the chest shield (rigid on `spine_05`) would go 56 cm through the floor in the bent-over slam, so the torso is
     straightened up to 45 deg and the pelvis raised up to 9 cm until it clears by 3 cm (smoothed over time);
   - the rigid gun arm and the fists would go 62 cm through the floor, so both arms are swung forward at the
     shoulder by the same angle (up to 114 deg, arm shape kept) until they clear by 3 cm. At the impact the gun arm
     lies forward, just off the floor.
6. Fists curled where the rig has finger weights (Grunt / Heavy).
7. Exported, then verified by reimport (above).

The scripts use absolute scratch paths (`/tmp/wd_atk/...`, `/tmp/wt-enemy-attacks/...`) from the build box; change
them to rebuild. `scripts/build_grunt.sh` builds the Grunt (`scripts/grunt.py`); `scripts/build.py` builds the others.

## Caveats (honest)
- **Grunt speed:** the authored speed is **303.5 cm/s**; the Grunt's `MoveSpeed` is 560 cm/s. Played in place at 1.0
  the feet will slide; matching needs a play rate of about 1.85 (which makes the cadence very fast), or a lower charge
  speed. A run with no flight phase tops out near 3 m/s for this body size, so "always a foot down" and 560 cm/s
  don't fit together; the root-motion variant shows the true speed.
- **Grunt mesh:** `SK_NullGrunt` has skin-weighting "fins" at the elbows and knees that show on any bend (checked on
  the bare mesh with a test bend, independent of this clip). It's an asset issue for the character owner; I didn't
  touch the character.
- **Heavy end pose:** the source's recovery doesn't step all the way back; the clip ends with the pelvis about
  41 cm from its start (mostly sideways, ~10 cm forward) and both feet re-set. Use a 0.2-0.3 s blend-out or cut at
  the end of `RecoverLeft` (frame 31); a pop or a short slide is possible when blending back to idle.
- **Heavy shield:** the Heavy's shield is part of the mesh on the left forearm; the bash is led by the left shoulder
  and forearm. Peak shield speed (frame 18) is 4 frames before the code's damage tick (frame 22); the shield is at
  near-full extension on frame 22.
- **Warden slam reads as a hunched hammer-fist slam, not hands on the floor:** the rigid chest shield and the rigid
  gun arm can't go through the floor, so the guards above lift the torso and swing the arms forward. At frame 40 the
  hands are 66 cm up and settle to 38 cm by frame 46; the gun barrel is ~3 cm off the floor. In the deep crouch the
  long rigid shield sticks out behind/below the torso (asset geometry). The torso extension (45 deg) and arm swing
  (114 deg) are large corrections - check the silhouette in game.
- **Warden timing:** the impact comes earlier in game if the charge hits a pawn (`EndCharge` fires on hit), and the
  capsule slides up to 12 m during frames 25-40 while the clip stays in place (arms overhead). The overhead hold
  covers the charge; there is no running in the clip.
- An earlier slam pick (`slamA_s6101`, hands on the floor, torso past horizontal) was dropped because the guards
  made it unreadable on the Warden; four more slam generations (slamC/slamD) were made with an upright-squat prompt.
- **Not tested in Unreal.** No Unreal processes were started for this delivery.
