## Skeleton and retarget (SMPL-H → SKEL_Pilot)
The clips are **not** on Manny/Quinn names. `SK_Pilot` / `SKEL_Pilot` is a 74-bone UE4-mannequin skeleton
(plus a `root` bone added on import, making 75 in the exported Pilot FBX). Suggested map:

| SMPL-H | SKEL_Pilot |
|---|---|
| Pelvis | pelvis |
| Spine1, Spine2, Spine3 | spine_01, spine_02, spine_03 |
| Neck, Head | neck_01, head |
| L_Collar, L_Shoulder, L_Elbow, L_Wrist | clavicle_l, upperarm_l, lowerarm_l, hand_l |
| R_Collar, R_Shoulder, R_Elbow, R_Wrist | clavicle_r, upperarm_r, lowerarm_r, hand_r |
| L_Hip, L_Knee, L_Ankle, L_Foot | thigh_l, calf_l, foot_l, ball_l |
| R_Hip, R_Knee, R_Ankle, R_Foot | thigh_r, calf_r, foot_r, ball_r |
| L_Index1/2/3, L_Middle1/2/3, L_Ring1/2/3, L_Pinky1/2/3, L_Thumb1/2/3 | index_01_l / _02_l / _03_l, and the same pattern for middle, ring, pinky, thumb; right side uses `_r` |

`SKEL_Pilot` also has `head_end`, `headfront`, `Bone_026`–`Bone_033` (eye chains) and `*_end` leaf bones
that have no SMPL-H source. There is no weapon socket in this FBX; the right hand is a closed sword grip
and there is no blade prop.

Facing: after Blender's default FBX import the character faces **-Y** (front toward a camera on -Y
looking at the origin). Dash and heavy-lunge travel is along **-Y** (forward). Finals were exported
with the same `axis_forward='-Z'`, `axis_up='Y'` as that importer default, so raw and final should
match. The Pilot mesh in Unreal faces **+Y** (mannequin convention), with `MeshYaw = -90` to face +X.
Builders should confirm forward after retarget.

## Units and export
- Raw fal FBX is centimetre-authored; Blender imports it at 0.01 object scale (metres in the viewport,
  character ~170 cm tall, Pelvis ~97 cm). Finals were re-exported from that scene with Apply Unit Scale
  + FBX All so they should come back the same way.
- A Blender FBX round-trip shifts the first frame from 1 to 2 (e.g. a 36-frame clip reimports as
  frames 2-37). Duration in seconds is unchanged. Hit frames below are **1-based from the start of
  the clip**, not from the reimported frame numbers.

## Rejected seeds (not in `source/`; preview strips are under `previews/candidates/`)
Each clip had two Fast seeds and one full seed, plus Light 1's existing seed 42 and three Air slam retries.
Losers, in short:

- Light 1: fast 101 (turns and runs), fast 102 (punch then runs), full 103 (too small a motion).
- Light 2: fast 201 (weaker rise), full 203 (almost idle; rejected as too weak).
- Light 3: fast 301 (messy), full 303 (more of a jab).
- Heavy lunge: fast 401 (a run, not a thrust), fast 402 (wild off-balance flail).
- Air slam first batch: fast 501/502 (dive / tuck), full 503 (spread jump). Retries full 511 (belly-flop) and 513 (tuck-dive).
- Block hold: fast 601/602 (raise then settle, not a hold).
- Deflect: full 703 (static crossed arms, no flick), fast 701 (usable, but 702 read better).
- Flourish: fast 802 (turns away), full 803 (walk-around).
- Dash: fast 901 (6.0 m), fast 902 (9.2 m) — real dashes, just longer than wanted.
- Hit front: fast 1001 (usable), full 1003 (too weak).
- Hit back: fast 1101/1102 (stumble out of frame).
- Death 1: fast 1201/1202 (fall leaves the frame).
- Death 2: fast 1301, full 1303 (leave the frame).
- Idle: fast 1401 (more pose drift), full 1403 (less of a blade guard, wanders).

## Prompts (verbatim)

Shared settings unless noted: `output_format=fbx`, `guidance_scale=7`, duration as in the table.
Light 1 seed 42 was an earlier test (guidance default 5, duration 1.2 s); its prompt was not recovered
from the test workspace. The r1 Light 1 prompt below was used for seeds 101, 102 and 103 only.
Air slam retries 511-513 used guidance 7.5 and the second prompt in that section.

### Light 1 (seeds 101, 102, 103; duration 1.2)
```
A person in a ready combat stance holds a one-handed sword in the right hand with a firm closed-fist sword grip, left hand free and raised in a guard. Starting in a mid guard with the sword held up at the right shoulder, they shift weight onto the left foot and perform a single sharp downward diagonal slash from high on the right to low on the left, cutting across the body at chest height. The hips and shoulders rotate into the swing, the right arm extends through the cut with clear follow-through, and the sword finishes low across the left hip. The body is slightly twisted to the left, weight on the left leg, right hand low, left hand still guarding. One short weighty slash only, no second swing, no running, no jumping.
```

### Light 2 (duration 1.2)
```
A person holds a one-handed sword in the right hand with a firm closed-fist sword grip, left hand free and raised in a guard. They start from a recovered pose after a downward slash: the sword is low across the left hip, the body is slightly twisted to the left, and the weight is on the left foot. They immediately cut back the other way with a single sharp rising diagonal slash from low left to high right, shifting weight onto the right foot, hips and shoulders rotating the other way. The right arm snaps the sword across the body with clear follow-through and finishes with the sword raised high on the right side, point slightly up, weight on the right leg, torso open, left hand still guarding. One slash only, no running, no jumping, no spin.
```

### Light 3 (duration 1.2)
```
A person holds a one-handed sword in the right hand with a firm closed-fist sword grip, left hand free and raised in a guard. They start with the sword already raised high on the right side after a rising cut, weight on the right foot. They step in slightly and deliver a powerful finishing downward slash from high on the right, over the shoulder, cutting nearly vertically down the centerline to about waist height, then recover into a balanced mid-guard ready stance facing forward, sword held in front of the right shoulder, weight even, left hand still guarding. One finishing slash only, no spin, no running, no jumping, clear follow-through then settle.
```

### Heavy lunge (duration 2.0)
```
A person in a mid-guard combat stance holds a one-handed sword in the right hand with a firm closed-fist sword grip, left hand free and raised in a guard. They wind up slowly for a long moment, coiling the hips and drawing the sword back to the right hip, then lunge far forward with a deep committed thrusting step, driving the sword straight forward in a powerful stab to chest height. After the thrust they hold the extended pose briefly and begin to recover. The body clearly travels forward with a large step. One heavy thrusting lunge only, no slash, no jump, no spin.
```

### Air slam, first batch (seeds 501-503; duration 2.0, guidance 7)
```
A person holding a one-handed sword in the right hand with a firm closed-fist sword grip, left hand free for balance, jumps upward from a ready stance, tucks slightly at the apex, then slams straight down and crashes to the ground on both feet in a heavy landing while swinging the sword downward in a vertical overhead smash that hits the ground in front of them as they land. The knees bend deeply on impact, the torso drops, then they recover to a low ready stance still holding the sword, left hand guarding. One jump and one downward slam only, no spin, no second swing.
```

### Air slam, retries (seeds 511-513, pick 512; duration 2.0, guidance 7.5)
```
A person holding a one-handed sword in the right hand, left hand free for balance, jumps straight up from a ready stance and stays facing forward. At the top of a short jump they raise the sword overhead, then they come straight down and land hard on both feet while swinging the sword in a vertical overhead smash that hits the ground in front of them at the same moment they land. Knees bend deeply on impact. They do not run forward, they do not dive, they do not roll, they do not spin. After the slam they recover to a low ready stance still holding the sword. One short jump and one downward smash only.
```

### Block hold (duration 2.0)
```
A person stands in a firm defensive block, holding a one-handed sword in the right hand with a closed-fist sword grip. The sword is held diagonally in front of the body from low left to high right at chest and face height, the left hand is raised as a guard, knees slightly bent, weight even. They hold this blocking pose with only a slight breathing motion and a tiny weight shift. No stepping, no attacking, no lowering the sword, no walking. The starting pose and the ending pose are the same so the motion can loop seamlessly.
```

### Deflect flick (duration 1.0)
```
A person holds a one-handed sword in the right hand in a high diagonal block in front of the body, left hand raised as a guard. They quickly flick the sword in a short sharp parry to the right, knocking an incoming strike aside with a snappy wrist and forearm motion, then immediately return the sword to the same high block. Small, tight, readable motion, almost in place, no stepping away, no spinning, no attacking slash, left hand stays in a guard.
```

### Flourish (duration 2.0)
```
A person holds a one-handed sword in the right hand in a high block, left hand raised as a guard. After a successful parry they perform a stylish flourish: a quick circular spin of the sword around the right wrist and a short confident turn of the shoulders, then snap back into a proud mid-guard ready stance facing forward, sword at the right shoulder, left hand guarding. Flashy but short and controlled, no full-body spin, no running, no jumping, weighty and readable from the front.
```

### Dash (duration 1.0)
```
A person holding a one-handed sword in the right hand in a mid-guard, left hand raised as a guard, suddenly dashes forward in a short explosive combat step. They lean forward, take one long traveling stride and a second quick step, sword held back and ready, then brake into a ready stance. Fast, low, athletic forward dash, not a jump, not a spin, clearly traveling forward, still holding the sword in the right hand.
```

### Hit react front (duration 1.2)
```
A standing person holding a one-handed sword in the right hand, left hand raised in a guard, is struck hard in the chest from the front. The torso snaps backward, the head jerks back, they stagger one small step back, then recover into a painful ready stance still holding the sword. Short hit reaction, no falling down, no dropping the sword, the left hand flinches then returns to guard.
```

### Hit react back (duration 1.2)
```
A standing person holding a one-handed sword in the right hand, left hand raised in a guard, is struck hard from behind in the upper back. The torso lurches forward, they stumble one small step forward, then recover and turn slightly back to a ready stance still holding the sword. Short hit reaction, no falling down, no dropping the sword.
```

### Death 1 (duration 2.5)
```
A person holding a one-handed sword in the right hand is fatally struck in the chest from the front, staggers backward, the sword arm goes limp, they collapse and fall onto their back, and they end lying still on the ground. No getting up, no writhing after they land.
```

### Death 2 (duration 2.5)
```
A person holding a one-handed sword in the right hand is fatally struck from the side, they spin halfway as they crumple and fall onto their left side, the sword dropping with the right arm, and they end lying still on the ground. No getting up, no writhing after they land.
```

### Idle (duration 3.0)
```
A person stands in a relaxed but alert combat idle, holding a one-handed sword in the right hand at a mid-guard beside the right shoulder as if the blade is ignited and lit, left hand raised in a loose guard. They slowly shift weight from one foot to the other, breathe, and make small adjustments of the sword and shoulders. No attacking, no walking away, no lowering the sword to the ground. The starting pose and the ending pose are the same so the motion can loop seamlessly.
```
