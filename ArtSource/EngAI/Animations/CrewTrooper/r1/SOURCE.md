## Skeleton, axes, units, root
- Bone names and hierarchy are exactly `SK_NullTrooper`'s 88 bones (UE5 Manny names, `pelvis` ... `ball_r`, metacarpals,
  twist bones, `ik_*`, `interaction`, `center_of_mass`). The armature node is named `root`, as in `SK_NullTrooper.fbx`, so
  it imports as the `root` bone. Import onto **SKEL_NullCrew** directly (no retarget needed); `SKEL_Warden` lists it as
  compatible.
- Twist bones are left at their rest pose relative to their parents (not driven). `ik_hand_gun` and `ik_hand_r` follow
  `hand_r`, `ik_hand_l` follows `hand_l`, `ik_foot_l/r` follow the feet, in world space, every frame.
- Facing and axes match `SK_NullTrooper.fbx` (Blender exporter defaults, `-Z` forward / `Y` up; the character faces -Y in
  Blender). Exported with Apply Unit Scale + "FBX All": **UnitScaleFactor 100**, data in metres, root scale 1. Verified by
  re-import (table below): pelvis 88-98 cm when standing, scale 1.0.
- **Root motion:** the horizontal pelvis travel of the clips that move (Alert, Cover_Enter, both hit reacts, all four deaths)
  is on the `root` bone (smoothed, Z = 0, no rotation). Loops, Fire_Burst and Reload have a static root. With root motion
  off in Unreal the body still travels with the root, exactly like the raw motion; with it on, the capsule follows.
- Scale: the motion is mapped by leg length (x1.098: SMPL-H ~170 cm to the 185 cm trooper).

## Clean-up done in Blender (per clip settings in `scripts/configs.json`)
1. **Retarget** SMPL-H to `SKEL_NullCrew`: each mapped bone copies the source bone's world rotation change from its rest
   pose, after aligning the target's A-pose bone directions to the source's T-pose directions (so the arms do not keep the
   A/T-pose difference). Spine: Spine1/2/3 -> spine_02/03/05; neck_02, spine_01/04 follow their parents.
2. **Floor contact:** each frame the body is lowered or raised so the lowest point of the `SK_NullTrooper` mesh touches
   Z = 0 (5-frame smoothing). Kneeling clips average that with the boot soles (see caveats).
3. **No foot sliding:** foot contacts are detected (ankle low and slow while the pelvis is above 55 cm, or locked for the
   whole clip where the feet never move) and pinned with a two-bone leg IK, blended over 4 frames at each end.
4. **Closed rifle grips:** a virtual rifle rides on `spine_05`; both hands are solved onto it with two-bone arm IK (right
   hand on the pistol grip, left hand under the handguard, about 18-28 cm apart), and all fingers are posed in a closed grip
   with the right index finger on the trigger. Rifle carry modes: aim (shouldered, level), low ready (-38°), muzzle up
   (cover). The left hand is released for the reload; both hands release during the deaths' fall.
5. **Military weight:** 4-10° of extra forward lean on the aim / fire / ready clips (HY-Motion stands slightly
   back-leaning); three recoil kicks added to Fire_Burst at the code's burst timing.
6. **Loops:** the last 12 frames are blended toward frame 0 so the last frame equals the first (Idle_Ready 0.27 cm, Idle_Aim
   0.12 cm, Cover_Idle 0.03 cm largest bone difference at the seam, measured on the re-imported FBX).

## Honest caveats (please check these in the capture)
- **Nobody has watched these at full speed in the game.** Previews are Blender workbench renders of `SK_NullTrooper`.
- **No rifle exists in game yet** (the Coilgun rifle is still on the request list). The red rifle in the sheets is a
  preview prop on `hand_r`; the hand placement assumes a compact rifle (grip to handguard ~25 cm). When the real rifle
  lands, a builder may need to nudge the left hand (or use `ik_hand_l` with the weapon's socket).
- **Alert ends hunched** (a crouched aim, chest pitched well forward) and about 49 cm away from where it started (root motion), so
  it does not end on the `Idle_Aim` pose: blend 0.2-0.3 s.
- **Kneeling (Cover_Enter end, Cover_Idle):** the trooper's shin armour is thicker than the generator's body; to keep both
  boots on the floor, the shin armour dips **about 5 cm below the floor** while kneeling. Cover_Enter ends 7 cm lower
  (pelvis 49 vs 56 cm) than Cover_Idle starts: blend 0.2 s.
- **Deaths travel far** (0.8-1.7 m of root motion; Death_Right is the longest) and end lying flat; Death_Left and
  Death_Right end more on the back than on the side. The lying body rests on the mesh's lowest point, so armour plates may
  clip up to 4 cm into the floor on some frames. The death clips assume the grip is released:
  there is no rifle-drop animation.
- **Hit react back** hunches very deep (head near waist height at the peak, frames 8-15): readable, but big.
- Fingers are a fixed closed grip driven by the hand (not animated from the raw clip); twist bones are not driven.
- Reload is generic: the left hand travels to the belt and back, but there is no magazine prop and no exact mag-out /
  mag-in frame was authored.
- HY-Motion's 'fire' generation had one big kick instead of three, so Fire_Burst is built from the Idle_Aim raw clip
  (frames 0-47) with procedural kicks; the rejected raw fire candidates are not committed.
