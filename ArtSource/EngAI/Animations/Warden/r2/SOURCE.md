## Skeleton, units, root
- **Skeleton:** the in-game `SK_Warden` / `SKEL_Warden` bones (`ArtSource/EngCharacters/Warden/export/SK_Warden.fbx`, 88 bones, UE5 Manny names, armature object `root`). These are not on the Rodin skeleton of #25's `mesh_r1/A_Warden_*.fbx`.
- **Units:** FBX 7.4, `UnitScaleFactor` **100**, Y-up, 30 fps. r1 (#87) was written at UnitScaleFactor 1.0 to match the skeleton file; both import to the same size in Unreal (the importer converts units). This delivery matches the #86 / #91 convention. Pelvis height after a Blender re-import of these files is 130-138 cm on a 260 cm body (scale factor ×1.618 from SMPL-H).
- **Root motion:** only the stagger has travel on the `root` bone (smoothed, Z = 0, no rotation). The roar has a static root. With root motion off in Unreal the staggering Warden still travels with the root; with it on, the capsule follows.
- Twist bones stay at rest relative to their parents. IK bones follow the hands and feet, same as r1.

## Clean-up done in Blender (`scripts/retarget.py`, per-clip settings in `scripts/configs.json`)
1. **Retarget** SMPL-H to `SKEL_Warden` by world-rotation transfer with a per-bone rest alignment (same method as #87 / #91).
2. **Floor contact** so the mesh approaches Z = 0 (a small clamp remains; see caveats).
3. **Foot IK** on detected planted contacts (0.0 cm planted slide on those intervals).
4. Root-motion extraction on the stagger; in-place root on the roar.
5. Exported keyed from time 0, mesh included for preview. Reimport checks are in `verify.json` / `metrics.json`.

## Picks (why these takes)
- Stagger: seed 8102 over 8101 (left foot almost always up), 8103 (more sideways than back) and 8104 (a spinning almost-fall). 8102 travels backward, stays planted (47 frames with a foot down), and recovers to the same wide stance and pelvis height it started from.
- Roar: seed 8204 over 8201 (a forward lunge, not a roar), 8202 (80 cm of travel, a 637 cm/s hitch) and 8203 (a 24 m/s arm snap on the first frames). 8204 is almost in place, arms go wide, then the fists come down into a forward ready stance, which matches the second-phase "battle cry" brief.

## Caveats
- **Stagger mid-pose.** Around frame 31 the torso folds quite deep (pelvis 110 cm) before it rises. It does recover to a planted stand and does not go to the ground. If the Lead wants a shallower hit, say so and a tighter take can be generated.
- **Roar vs summon.** The first half (arms wide, head up) is the roar; the second half is a forward lean into a ready stance, not a second roar. It should not be confused with r1 `Summon_01`.
- **Floor.** The mesh dips up to about 1.2 cm under Z = 0 on some frames. The Warden's soles sit around 24 cm (the larger boots).
- **UnitScaleFactor 100**, not 1.0. See the units note above. Re-export with Apply Unit Scale off / the r1 settings if the importer needs 1.0.
- The authored shield and gun-arm stay on their sockets; this clip does not re-author the Nov 14 `lowerarm_l` shield bind, it just plays it.
