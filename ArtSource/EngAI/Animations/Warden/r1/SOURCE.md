## Cleanup done in Blender (`scripts/retarget.py`, per-clip settings in `scripts/clips.json`)
1. World-space rotation transfer SMPL-H -> SK_Warden per bone, with a per-bone swing alignment of rest poses,
   scaled to the 260 cm body (x1.62).
2. **Retime** with time-warp knots so the release lands exactly on frame 16 (0.5 s) - anticipation, then release,
   then a slower recovery and settle. The model's windups were sped up about 1.3-1.9x to fit the 0.5 s code windup;
   recoveries were slowed for weight.
3. Constant yaw correction so each clip faces forward (-Y); drift fix on the pelvis; `root` kept still (in place).
4. **Foot contacts:** detected and hand-set plant windows, feet IK-pinned (analytic two-bone leg IK) to the
   segment means, clean lifted arcs (8 cm) for the few re-set steps, soles raised 0.5-2.5 cm so they rest on the floor.
5. Fire clips: aim-pitch layer (+4 deg) so the gun arm is level at the release; Fire_02 authored recoil (see above).
6. Fire_01 starts with a 10-frame blend from the model's own end pose so start and end match.
7. Exported keyed from time 0; verified by reimport (`scripts/verify.py` -> `verify.json`): bone names / rest pose,
   frame count, 30 fps, UnitScaleFactor, first frame 1 after reimport, foot travel, lowest vertex per frame;
   `scripts/pops.py` checked per-frame bone rotation for snaps.

The scripts use absolute scratch paths (`/tmp/wd/...`) from the build box; change them to rebuild.

## Caveats (honest)
- **Grips:** `SK_Warden`'s finger bones have **no skin weights** (fingers are weighted to the hand), so finger poses
  can't show; the left hand stays the Rodin open claw and the right hand is the rigid gun arm. Closed fists aren't
  possible without re-skinning the hands. Finger bones are keyed from the source but have no visible effect.
- **Shield:** rigid on `spine_05`; in Summon_01's deepest hunch (frames 35-36) the shield's lower edge dips about 6 cm
  under the floor, and in frames 1-6 the left sole's edge (foot tilted for the step-turn) sits about 1.6 cm into the floor.
- **Summon_01** re-sets both feet with a small step-turn in its first ~0.6 s, and its end pose differs from its start
  (one-shot, not a loop), so check the blend from idle.
- **Summon_02** reads like a ground slam; it could be confused with ChargeSlam (`MM_ChargedAttack`). Summon_01 is the
  recommended summon; Summon_02 is the alternate (or a candidate for the slam).
- **Timing / weight:** the code's attack window is 0.5 s windup + 0.5 s recover = 1.0 s; the clips run 1.30-1.63 s,
  so the last 0.3-0.6 s of settle is only seen if the one-shot isn't cut at `FinishAttack`. The 0.5 s windup is short
  for a 260 cm boss; for more weight, T5 could raise `BurstWindup` to about 0.8 s and these could be re-timed in r2.
- Fast frames: the strongest per-frame rotation is the Summon_02 fist strike (about 80 deg/frame on the left hand at
  frames 13-14); everything else is under about 36 deg/frame.
- **Not tested in Unreal.** No Unreal processes were started for this delivery.
