## Checks (2026-11-13)
All four delivery maps are 2048×2048. Seamless test: mean absolute difference of opposite edges versus the mean absolute difference of interior neighbouring rows/columns; wrap-around error was comparable to the interior neighbour step on every map (pass). Ranges are sensible (normal Z high and outward, ORM metallic at 0, height and base colour have real variation). No map failed.

## Import notes for the materials builder
- Unreal: BC default / sRGB on; N as Normalmap, **DirectX**; ORM as Masks, sRGB off (R AO, G roughness, B metallic); H grayscale, sRGB off, for displacement / parallax.
- Suggested near-scale tiling: one repeat = 400 cm (4 m).

## Notes
The orange-red in the deep cracks is baked into the base colour only. An emissive mask could be derived later by the materials builder.
