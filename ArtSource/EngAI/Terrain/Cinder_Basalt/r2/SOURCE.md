## Checks (2026-11-13)
All four delivery maps are 2048×2048. Seamless test: mean absolute difference of opposite edges versus the mean absolute difference of interior neighbouring rows/columns; wrap-around error was comparable to the interior neighbour step on every map (BC left-right / top-bottom ratios 1.14 / 1.07, N 1.07 / 1.10, ORM 1.00 / 1.10, H 0.78 / 1.10; all pass). Ranges are sensible (normal Z mean 0.962, min 0.651, high and outward; ORM metallic at 0; height and base colour have real variation). No map failed.

## Import notes for the materials builder
- Unreal: BC default / sRGB on; N as Normalmap, **DirectX**; ORM as Masks, sRGB off (R AO, G roughness, B metallic); H grayscale, sRGB off, for displacement / parallax.
- Suggested near-scale tiling: one repeat = 400 cm (4 m).
- This r2 folder is the intended replacement for the r1 maps at `ArtSource/EngAI/Terrain/Cinder_Basalt/` (those files are left in place; do not overwrite them).
