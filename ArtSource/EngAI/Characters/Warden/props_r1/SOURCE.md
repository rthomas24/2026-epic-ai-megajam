**FBX — preferred for Unreal.** Exported with the repo's Unreal convention
(`apply_unit_scale=True`, `apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_UNITS'`, `axis_forward='-Z'`,
`axis_up='Y'`, face smoothing, tangents, triangulated). The file's
`UnitScaleFactor` / `OriginalUnitScaleFactor` are **100** (centimetres). Import with the usual
Interchange pipeline, uniform scale **1.0**; the shield should measure 181.0 cm tall and the
gun arm 122.6 cm long in Unreal.

**GLB.** Vertex values are in **centimetres**, with `export_yup=false`, so the file itself is
+Z up / +X forward rather than glTF's default +Y up / +Z forward. glTF's official unit is the
metre: if an importer assumes metres, set uniform scale to **0.01**; if it treats the numbers as
cm (or you are in Unreal and want the centimetre sizes above), use scale **1.0**. The GLB embeds
the OpenGL (glTF) normal, plus BC, metallic-roughness and emissive.

## Normal convention

`source/*/texture_normal.png` (and the normal embedded in the derived GLB) is **OpenGL** (Y+):
the B-channel is nearly 255 (outward Z), and the glTF 2.0 spec requires OpenGL tangent-space
normals. That matches how Rodin ships a glTF pack.

For Unreal, which expects **DirectX** (Y−), the delivery maps `T_*_N.png` have the green
channel inverted (`G' = 1 - G`). Red and blue are unchanged. This is the same convention used
for the accepted Drone mesh_r1, the player-ship mesh_r1 and the Warden mesh_r1. When importing
the loose maps, set N as a Normalmap (DirectX) and do **not** also use the GLB-embedded OpenGL
normal.

## Notes and issues

- The **front of the shield and the gun arm are faithful** to the approved r2 isolated concepts: black armour, red-orange seams, chevron face, six-barrel cannon with a cuff at the elbow and a glowing sight.
- The **back of the shield** was not in the concept (the painting is a front 3/4 of the face). Rodin invented a rust-red / brown back plate and a rectangular grip loop. The grip is usable and is where the pivot sits, but the back colour does not match the black armour of the face. Builders may want to repaint the back, or leave it (it faces the Warden).
- The grip is slightly off the back-face midline, as modeled; the pivot is at that grip, not at the geometric centre of the plate.
- No sockets, no UCX collision — please add those on import. This drop was not run through `blender-ai-mesh-cleanup`.
- Topology is Rodin's auto-triangulation (not clean quads). Tris are already well under any likely prop budget.
