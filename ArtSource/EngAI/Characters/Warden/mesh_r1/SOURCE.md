## Raw source inspection

Inspected on 2026-11-13 with Blender 4.5.3 (headless). Two Rodin downloads were kept, both untouched:

- `source/base_pack/` — the Base Model pack (`warden_body_base_pbr.zip`): `base.fbx`,
  `base_basic_pbr.glb`, `base_basic_shaded.glb`, `texture_emissive.png`. This pack does **not**
  include the loose albedo / normal / ORM maps; those arrived with the rigged export. The pack's
  `base.fbx` already contains the 52-bone armature and all seven animation takes (it is not a
  mesh-only file). The GLB is the unrigged mesh.
- `source/rigged_pack/` — the rigged animation export (`warden_body_rigged_animations_7clips.zip`):
  `base.fbx` (mesh + rig + seven takes, no embedded PBR), `base_basic_pbr.fbx`,
  `base_basic_shaded.fbx`, and loose 2K `texture_{diffuse,normal,metallic,roughness,pbr,emissive}.png`
  plus `shaded.png`. This is the pack the derived files were built from
  (`source/rigged_pack/base.fbx`).
- `source/warden_body_viewer.png` — the Rodin viewer still of the confirmed body.

The two `base.fbx` files have the same bone hierarchy and the same seven take IDs but are not
byte-identical.

### Islands in the raw mesh

The raw body is not one connected component. After import, five islands:

- 20,683 verts — the body, including the **welded gun arm** (the cannon replaces the right forearm
  and hand, as in the A-pose concept).
- 4,285 verts — the **shield**, already a disconnected island (left of the character, mid-torso
  height). It is not parented; it is only a loose shell with its own skin weights.
- three 4-vert floaters (millimetre-scale) near the right shoulder / helmet / gun. Removed on the
  derived meshes only; `source/` is untouched.

The approved r2 A-pose still shows the shield and the gun on the character (the isolated shield and
gun paintings were meant as later, separate-mesh references). Rodin therefore baked the gun into
the body. The shield could be split cleanly because it was already a separate island. A later
shield / gun PR from the isolated concept paintings can still replace these.

## Normal convention

`source/rigged_pack/texture_normal.png` is **OpenGL** (Y+): the B-channel is nearly 255 (outward Z),
and the glTF 2.0 spec requires OpenGL tangent-space normals. That matches how Rodin ships a glTF pack.

For Unreal, which expects **DirectX** (Y−), the delivery map `T_Warden_N.png` has the green channel
inverted (`G' = 1 - G`). Red and blue are unchanged. This is the same convention used for the
accepted Drone mesh_r1 and the player-ship mesh_r1. When importing the loose maps, set N as a
Normalmap (DirectX).

## Derived-only edits (source untouched)

1. **Tiny islands.** Three 4-vert floaters (12 verts total) were deleted from the derived mesh.
2. **Decimate.** Blender Collapse decimate, UVs preserved, to 39,600 triangles (ratio ≈ 0.792 from
   49,988 after the floater cleanup). Topology is still Rodin's auto-triangulation, not clean quads.
3. **Shield split.** The disconnected shield island was separated into `SK_Warden_Shield` /
   `SM_Warden_Shield`. It keeps its auto-skin weights (not a rigid child of the left hand).
4. **Reorient, scale, feet pivot.** +90° about Z (−Y front → +X), uniform scale to 260 cm, origin at
   the feet centre. Animations were kept by parenting the armature to a transform empty, applying
   the object transform, then scaling Hips location keys so root motion matches the new scale.

After decimate, a few verts picked up more than 8 influences (body: 11 verts at 9–10; shield: 5
verts at 9–10). Raw max was 8. Unreal's default influence cap is often 8; the animation builder may
want to clamp and renormalise those 16 verts on import. Weight-sum error remains ≤ 0.00049. No
unweighted verts.

## Rig assessment (honest)

The **bind / A-pose mesh is solid** and close to the approved r2 concept: black armour, red-orange
seams, glowing white eyes, plain chest chevron, no mid-chest badge. Builders should not be blocked
on the mesh.

The **auto-rig is usable for a jam / first pass, and is weak at extremes.** In order:

- **Idle and Walk** read correctly. The shield is not held in a combat guard in these clips (it
  sits beside / behind the shoulder) because the library clips are generic humanoid, not authored
  for a tower shield.
- **Stagger** is a hunched, guarded travel walk with the shield high. Readable.
- **Death** is a kneel-and-collapse onto the front. Some mesh intersection with the shield once
  prone, but the move is clear.
- **Shoulder Hit And Fall** is a knock-back tumble onto the back / side. Mid-air frames have some
  clipping.
- **Slam** (Mutant Jump Attack) is clearly a leap-and-slam, but the impact frame crushes the mesh
  and the body dips about 40 cm through the ground. The shield warps on the way down (soft
  auto-skin on a part that should be rigid).
- **Rifle Stealth Walk** is obviously a crouched two-hand rifle walk retargeted onto this
  character, so the shield is held out like a gun and **warps badly**. It is not a fire clip.
- The **gun arm is welded** to the right forearm. Finger / hand bones in a "rifle hold" or a
  clenched pose can tug the barrels slightly. A later separate gun-arm mesh from the isolated
  concept will be cleaner if the builders want it detachable.
- The shield should eventually be a rigid child of the left hand (or a socketed static mesh), not a
  softly skinned shell.

The Lead's instruction stands: the clean A-pose mesh can go in now; the animation builder should
plan to clean foot sliding, convert travel clips to in-place if needed, tighten the slam landing,
and replace or retarget fire / summon (and probably the stealth-walk, unless a crouched "rifle"
move is wanted).

## Import notes for the builders

- Target slot: `Gameplay/Characters/` new `DA_Crew_Warden_Visuals`.
- Unreal: prefer the FBX at uniform scale 1.0. BC default / sRGB on; N as Normalmap, **DirectX**
  (`T_Warden_N.png`); ORM as Masks, sRGB off (R AO, G roughness, B metallic); E as emissive, sRGB on.
- Pivot is at the feet, on the ground, centred on the soles. First check: 260.0 cm tall, facing
  world +X, feet on the actor origin, no extra root scale.
- `SK_Warden.fbx` has two meshes on one skeleton. Import as a single skeletal mesh with two
  sections, or take only the body and socket the `SM_Warden_Shield` (or the later dedicated shield)
  to the left hand.
- No sockets, no UCX collision, no physics asset — please add those on import. This drop was not
  run through `blender-ai-mesh-cleanup`.
- Skeleton is the Rodin Human template, not UE5 Manny (52 bones, no extra root, no twist bones).
  The animation builder will need an IK Retargeter if Manny / Quinn clips are to play on it.
- Suggested animation start: Idle and Walk as-is (Watch root motion), then decide whether to keep,
  clean or replace Slam / Death / Stagger. Do not treat Rifle Stealth Walk as fire.
