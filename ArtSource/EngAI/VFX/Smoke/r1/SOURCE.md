## Pack
- ffmpeg extracted all 150 frames. The best-matching loop window in the search was the
  full clip (frames 0–149), subsampled to 64, with an 8-frame end/start crossfade.
- Alpha = `clip((luma - 4) * 1.15)` where luma is `0.299R+0.587G+0.114B`.
- Loop MSE remains noticeable (~3022 after the crossfade) because the plume evolves
  over the five seconds; the seam is softened, not eliminated.
