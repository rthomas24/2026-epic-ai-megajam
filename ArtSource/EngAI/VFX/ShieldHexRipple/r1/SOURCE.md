## Construction
Pointy-top hex tessellation. Hex size (centre-to-vertex) is `size/16`. Each
pixel is mapped to its containing hex (axial / cube rounding); signed distance
to that hex and to a large central hexagon use six half-planes (apothem =
R·√3/2).

- Wire + glow of the tessellation, stronger inside a soft disc and along the ripple.
- Main energy ring: Gaussian of the large-hex SDF at R = 0.34·size (still).
- Cells along the ring take a faint fill.
- A weaker circular ripple rides the same radius.
- Still also has a secondary inner hex ring at R = 0.18·size and a small centre flash.
- Colour: cyan-teal energy (white core, teal mid, cyan rim).

The 8×8 is 64 one-shot frames (rendered at 640 and downsampled to 256). The ring
radius eases from 0.05·size to 0.48·size; intensity fades after mid; a centre
flash dies in the first few cells. No source media folder is required.
