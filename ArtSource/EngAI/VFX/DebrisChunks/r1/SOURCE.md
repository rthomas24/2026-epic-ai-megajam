## Pack
- The 2048 grid was sliced into 16 cells of 512×512.
- In each cell, a max-RGB threshold just above the black background (corner p99
  was ~3, threshold 10) produced a binary mask. Connected components were
  kept if they were the main chunk or reasonably large spark flecks.
- Each kept chunk was recentred in the cell with a 16 px margin (scaled down
  only if it would not fit). Alpha is opaque on the metal mask and uses a
  soft max-RGB ramp for the molten glow. Dark metal stays nearly opaque
  (mean alpha ~250 on mid-dark metal pixels). Cell seams are fully transparent.
