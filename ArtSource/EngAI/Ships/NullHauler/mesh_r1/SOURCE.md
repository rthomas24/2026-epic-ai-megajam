metadata; glTF's default unit is the metre. `source/base_basic_pbr.fbx` was the
inspected source.

called out as sensor domes on Nicholas's board. They are not a hole, a missing half, or a
major distortion, so they were left in place for this r1; flag them if they should come off
in a later pass.

## Derived delivery (what to import)

Cleaned, reoriented, pivoted, scaled and decimated in Blender 4.5.3. Source files were not
modified.

1. Removed **5 detached micro-fragments** (60 faces) whose minimum distance to the hull was
   greater than ~10 cm at delivery scale. Three other 4-face chips sitting on the surface
   were kept. Cargo containers stayed as separate components (they are the modular crates,
   not junk). The hull stayed watertight.
2. Recalculated normals consistently, outward (volume proxy positive before and after scale).
3. Rotated **+90° about Z** so the nose points **+X** (raw −Y → +X). Quad thrusters point
   **−X**. +Z stays up. +Y is starboard.
4. Uniformly scaled so overall length (`max X − min X`) is **40.0 m (4000.0 cm)**, matching
   `ATDArmadaConvoy`'s 40 m freighter scale, then centred the bounding box on the origin.
   Aspect ratio was **not** squashed to the 13 × 11 m placeholder box — the swept wings and
   hexagonal sensor pods make the natural beam wider, and height follows the same uniform
   scale.
5. A 0.1 mm `remove_doubles` weld was trialled and **reverted** (it introduced 10 boundary
   and 5 non-manifold edges). No weld is applied in the delivery.
6. `DECIMATE` `COLLAPSE` (UVs kept, `use_collapse_triangulate=True`) at ratio **0.5306**,
   landing at **26,500 tris** before export (inside 24,000–28,000, strictly under 30,000).
   The exported FBX reimports at **26,497 tris**. UVs stay in 0–1. Topology stays watertight.

The placeholder in `ATDArmadaConvoy::SetupLane` is a 40 × 13 × 11 m box. This mesh matches the
40 m length and keeps the natural proportions of Nicholas's board, so it is 19.40 m wide and
8.76 m tall once the wings and pods are included.

**FBX (`SM_NullHauler.fbx`) — preferred for Unreal.** Exported with the repo's Unreal convention
(`apply_unit_scale=True`, `apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_UNITS'`, `axis_forward='-Z'`,
`axis_up='Y'`, face smoothing, tangents, triangulated). The file's `UnitScaleFactor` /
`OriginalUnitScaleFactor` are **100** (centimetres). Import at uniform scale **1.0**; it
measures 4000.0 cm long in Unreal. The file also contains `SOCKET_*` empties and five
`UCX_SM_NullHauler_00..04` boxes (core hull, nose, stern, starboard wing, port wing). Gameplay
currently sets convoy meshes to `NoCollision`; the UCX hulls are there for traces if Engineering
wants them.

**GLB (`SM_NullHauler.glb`).** Vertex values are in **centimetres**, with `export_yup=false`, so
the file itself is +Z up / +X forward. Accessor min/max is X −2000..+2000, Y −970.0..+969.9,
Z −437.8..+438.5. Blender's glTF importer assumes Y-up and will appear to swap Y/Z on reimport;
the file on disk is Z-up.
