## Rebuild
```
python3 source/make_textures.py /tmp/dm/tex4k /tmp/dm/tex2k
# preview/GLB normal: OpenGL copy of N (G inverted) -> /tmp/dm/tex2k/T_DominionMothership_N_OpenGL.png
blender -b --factory-startup --python source/build_dominion_mothership.py -- /tmp/dm/tex2k /tmp/dm/export
blender -b --factory-startup --python source/verify_fbx.py -- /tmp/dm/export /tmp/dm/tex2k /tmp/dm/verify.json
```
The build is deterministic (seeded RNG), so a rebuild gives the same tri count (87,756) and UCX set (59).

## Axis convention
Geometry is authored in Unreal axes and mirrored in Y once before export. The repo's FBX convention (`axis_forward='-Z'`, `axis_up='Y'`,
`apply_unit_scale=True`, `FBX_SCALE_UNITS`, as in Bastion/Lancer mesh_r1) maps Blender (x, y, z) m to Unreal (100x, -100y, 100z) cm.
So starboard (+Y Unreal) is Blender -Y, and socket yaws are negated in Blender. `verify_fbx.py` reports every number back in Unreal cm.
**Please confirm handedness on the first Unreal import:** `SOCKET_HullTurret_1` should be on the starboard (+Y) deck edge near the bow
(X +18000), and `SOCKET_Generator_0` at (+27500, +27500, 0) should face yaw 45°.

## Texturing method
- One material and one UV set. The UVs are an **atlas / trim sheet** of 12 regions (light armour, dark gunmetal armour, trench floor, trench wall,
  red banner with trident, red trim, mechanical metal, amber light, white eye light, dark recess, engine core, hangar bulkhead). Each face is
  box-projected at a uniform density (48 m per half-atlas). Banners, engine cores and light strips are fitted per face. Faces are oriented so
  texels are never mirrored.
- Colours: armour #41464f–#525760 and #2d3138–#3c4048, recess/seams #16181c, banners #7c1414–#a31a1a with a white trident,
  amber #ff5a14, eye slits #fff4e6, engine cores white-hot → orange.
- N is DirectX: green is inverted relative to OpenGL. Flat areas are (128, 128, 255) (median measured), and the synthetic-bump green test passes.
  ORM has R = AO (cavity), G = roughness, B = metallic. E is thresholded so every pixel with maxRGB ≤ 18 becomes 0 (0 grey pixels remain).
- The 4096 masters are downsampled to 2048 with Lanczos; N is renormalised and E re-thresholded after the resize.
- The detail layer is an exactly periodic field (FFT noise, wrap-mode filters). Wrap-seam differences are 0.2 (N) / 0.2 (ORM) against interior
  neighbour differences of 0.7–1.0, so it tiles seamlessly.

## Known limits / notes for the importer
- Shading is flat (hard-surface, per-face normals) and the mesh is triangulated, so it is Nanite-ready.
- The trench turret sockets sit at |Y| 1700, inside the 50 m corridor, as `TDDominion.cpp` places them. The brackets stay on the wall (|Y| ≥ 2369),
  so the turret actors themselves overhang the corridor's upper edge at Z 7700.
- The generator plinth top is at Z -50 so the standalone generator dome has the space above Z 0. The current code placeholder cylinder
  (12 × 12 × 20 m, centred on the actor) would dip 10 m into the plinth until the generator art mesh replaces it.
- The renders are Blender Cycles previews. The Unreal material look (detail tiling, emissive strength) will differ.
