## Rebuild
```
python3 source/make_textures.py /tmp/dm_r2/tex4k /tmp/dm_r2/tex2k
# preview/GLB normal: OpenGL copy of N (G inverted) -> /tmp/dm_r2/tex2k/T_DominionMothership_N_OpenGL.png
blender -b --factory-startup --python source/build_dominion_mothership.py -- /tmp/dm_r2/tex2k /tmp/dm_r2/export
blender -b --factory-startup --python source/verify_fbx.py -- /tmp/dm_r2/export /tmp/dm_r2/tex2k /tmp/dm_r2/verify.json
```
The build is deterministic (seeded RNG 4101 for the r1 body, 4102 for r2 additions), so a rebuild gives the same tri count (131,670) and UCX set (121).

## Axis convention and FBX unit scale (audit D4)
Geometry is authored in Unreal axes and mirrored in Y once before export. The repo's FBX convention (`axis_forward='-Z'`, `axis_up='Y'`,
`apply_unit_scale=True`, `FBX_SCALE_UNITS`, as in Bastion/Lancer mesh_r1 and Dominion mesh_r1) maps Blender (x, y, z) m to Unreal (100x, -100y, 100z) cm.
`UnitScaleFactor` = 100 and `OriginalUnitScaleFactor` = 100 are set in the file; Unreal should import at uniform scale **1.0**.

**Blender re-import note (the D4 warning):** Blender's FBX importer displays the file in metres, so the overall bbox reads about **656.05 × 603.49 × 326.0** Blender units (engines / mandibles / outriggers / the Z 198 m mast). That is 65605 × 60349 × 32600 cm in Unreal, not a scale error. `verify_fbx.py` reports every number back in Unreal cm, and `import_object_scale` is `(1, 1, 1)`.

**Please confirm handedness on the first Unreal import:** `SOCKET_HullTurret_1` should be on the starboard (+Y) deck edge near the bow
(X +18000), and `SOCKET_Generator_0` at (+27500, +27500, 0) should face yaw 45°.

## Editable `.blend` (audit E / D4)
`SM_DominionMothership.blend` is the polishable source:
- Collections `Visual`, `Collision`, `Sockets`.
- The visual mesh keeps **unapplied** `Bevel` (limit = WEIGHT, 2 segments, width 0.32 m, harden normals, clamp overlap) and `WeightedNormal` (face-area, keep sharp), after `shade_smooth` + `set_sharp_from_angle(30°)`.
- Bevel weights are assigned only to long hard edges (length ≥ 8 m, adjacent face area ≥ 15 m², dihedral ≥ 40°) so window slits and greebles do not explode the triangle budget.
- 2K preview textures are packed. The FBX/GLB are exported from an evaluated, fully triangulated copy so tangent space can be written; that copy is not stored in the `.blend`.

## Texturing method
- One material and one UV set. The UVs are an **atlas / trim sheet** of 12 regions (light armour, dark gunmetal armour, trench floor, trench wall,
  crimson banner with trident, red trim, mechanical metal, amber light, white eye light, dark recess, engine core, hangar bulkhead). Each face is
  box-projected at a uniform density (48 m per half-atlas). Banners, engine cores and light strips are fitted per face. Faces are oriented so
  texels are never mirrored.
- Colours (r2): armour midtones lifted to about #4c525c–#646b76 and #3a3f48–#4e545e, recess/seams #16181c, banners #9a1620–#d42834 with a white trident,
  amber #ff5a14, eye slits #fff4e6, engine cores white-hot → orange.
- N is DirectX: green is inverted relative to OpenGL. Height-to-normal strength is 8.5 (atlas) / 5.5 (detail).
- The 4096 masters are downsampled to 2048 with Lanczos; N is renormalised and E re-thresholded after the resize.

## Known limits / notes for the importer
- The trench turret sockets sit at |Y| 1700, inside the 50 m corridor, as `TDDominion.cpp` places them. The brackets stay on the wall (|Y| ≥ 2369).
- The generator plinth top is at Z -50 so the standalone generator dome has the space above Z 0.
- New r2 mass stays outside the trench (mid-spine blocks at |Y| ≥ 34 m) or aft of X -180 (citadel stacks). Extra UCX for that mass do not enter the keep-clear volumes.
- The renders are Blender Cycles previews under Blender's bundled `studio.exr` HDRI. The Unreal material look (detail tiling, emissive strength) will differ.
