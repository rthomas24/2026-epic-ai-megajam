## Derived delivery (what to import)

Reoriented, recentred, scaled and decimated in Blender 4.5.3. Source files were not modified.

The raw 50,000-tri mesh was reduced with a Decimate (COLLAPSE, ratio 0.396,
`use_collapse_triangulate=True`) to **19,800 triangles**, inside the 12–20k cap and near
the top of the budget so the wall-like gunship keeps as much panel detail as the cap
allows. The UV layer was kept (UV-space area 0.666 → 0.620; a ~7% drop, no unwrap
was redone). Decimate nudged the bounding box by about 1%, so the mesh was then
rescaled so the length is exactly **1500.0 cm**.

|---|---|
| Extents | **1500.0 × 1432.0 × 1116.2 cm** (X × Y × Z)  (L × W × H) |
| vs brief | brief was ~1500 cm long, no width/height given; length was matched exactly after the decimate rescale |
| Uniform scale | 7.81243497 from the raw import (applied in metres, then exported) |
| Pivot | bounding-box centre at the origin |
| Axes | **+Z up**, **+X forward** (chevron prow / eye bar / twin turrets; engines at −X) |
| Tris | **19,800** (inside the 12–20k budget, after decimate from 50,000) |

**FBX (`SM_NullGunship.fbx`) — preferred for Unreal.** Exported with the repo's Unreal convention
(`apply_unit_scale=True`, `apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_UNITS'`, `axis_forward='-Z'`,
`axis_up='Y'`, face smoothing, tangents, triangulated). The file's `UnitScaleFactor` /
`OriginalUnitScaleFactor` are **100** (centimetres). Reimported in Blender it measures
15.000 × 14.320 × 11.162 m, centred on the origin. Import with the usual Interchange
pipeline, uniform scale **1.0**; it should measure 1500.0 cm long in Unreal.

**GLB (`SM_NullGunship.glb`).** Vertex values are in **centimetres**, with `export_yup=false`, so the
file itself is +Z up / +X forward rather than glTF's default +Y up / +Z forward. glTF's official unit
is the metre: if an importer assumes metres, set uniform scale to **0.01**; if it treats the numbers as
cm (or you are in Unreal and want 1500 uu), use scale **1.0**. The GLB embeds the OpenGL (glTF)
normal, plus BC, metallic-roughness and emissive.

**GLB caveat:** the raw POSITION accessor is slightly short of a perfect 1500 cm on +X
(min X = −750.0, max X = 747.4; length 1497.4 cm, about 1.3 cm off the origin). Y and Z
are centred and match the FBX. Prefer the FBX for the engine import; the loose maps are
the same either way.

## Lead-note check: red-orange seams and the white 'eye' bar

Checked on the 2K maps and on the derived-mesh renders (see `contact_sheet.png`).

- **Red-orange seams: present.** They read on the front chevron, the dorsal rail lines, the
  turret rings, the side-pod insets and the engine rims, in both `texture_diffuse.png` /
  `T_NullGunship_BC.png` and `texture_emissive.png` / `T_NullGunship_E.png`. The
  emissive has about 14,800 orange pixels (0.35% of the 2K map). They are clearly
  visible in the front, top and 3/4 contact-sheet views. The hull itself is a deep black
  (closer to the concept than the mid-grey Fighter), so the seams have good contrast.
- **White 'eye' bar: present.** The emissive has about 18,500 near-white pixels (0.44% of
  the map). On the mesh it reads as a row of white rectangular lamps across the front
  chevron, in the same place as the concept. It holds up in the front and 3/4 views.

## Notes and issues

- The silhouette is faithful: a wall-like blocky hull, a front chevron with a white eye
  bar, twin paired turrets on the foredeck, side engine pods, four rear engine bells,
  and forward landing / support legs. It clearly shares the Null / Triad family language
  with the Fighter (black armour, orange seams, white eyes, chevrons) as asked in R-034.
- The approved concept is followed closely. Small departures: some flank panel greeble
  is softer after the 50k→19.8k decimate (the side view is the weakest for crisp
  plating); the side pods are a little more bulbous than the concept; a few of the front
  orange seam lines are slightly messier than the concept's hard chevron.
- No rig, no sockets, no UCX collision — please add those on import if you want them. This
  drop was not run through `blender-ai-mesh-cleanup`.
- Topology is Rodin's auto-triangulation, then a collapse decimate (not clean quads).
- R-035 asked for a per-ship heat-glow mask matching these UVs; that mask is not in this
  drop and can follow in a new folder once scheduled.

## Normal convention

`source/texture_normal.png` (and the normal embedded in both the raw and the derived GLB) is
**OpenGL** (Y+): it is the glTF normal, B-channel is nearly 255 (outward Z), and the glTF 2.0 spec
requires OpenGL tangent-space normals. That matches how Rodin ships a glTF pack.

For Unreal, which expects **DirectX** (Y−), the delivery map `T_NullGunship_N.png` has the green
channel inverted (`G' = 1 - G`). Red and blue are unchanged. This is the same convention used for
the accepted Drone mesh_r1, the player-ship mesh_r1, the Warden mesh_r1 and the Null Fighter
mesh_r1. When importing the loose maps, set N as a Normalmap (DirectX) and do **not** also use the
GLB-embedded OpenGL normal.
