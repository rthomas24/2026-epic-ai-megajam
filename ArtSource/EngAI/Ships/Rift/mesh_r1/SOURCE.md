**FBX (`SM_Rift.fbx`) — preferred for Unreal.** Exported with the repo's Unreal convention
(`apply_unit_scale=True`, `apply_scale_options='FBX_SCALE_UNITS'`, `axis_forward='-Z'`,
`axis_up='Y'`, face smoothing, tangents, triangulated). The file's `UnitScaleFactor` /
`OriginalUnitScaleFactor` are **100** (centimetres). Import with the usual Interchange pipeline,
uniform scale **1.0**; it should measure 500.0 cm long in Unreal.

**GLB (`SM_Rift.glb`).** Vertex values are in **centimetres**, with `export_yup=false`, so the
file itself is +Z up / +X forward rather than glTF's default +Y up / +Z forward. glTF's official unit
is the metre: if an importer assumes metres, set uniform scale to **0.01**; if it treats the numbers as
cm (or you are in Unreal and want 500 uu), use scale **1.0**. The GLB embeds the OpenGL (glTF)
normal, plus BC, metallic-roughness and emissive.

## Portal ring (derived mesh only)

The raw Rodin mesh filled the portal-emitter ring with a solid magenta / emissive disc (a shallow
lens / membrane). The Lead's approval note requires an **open ring** (no solid disc) so engineering
can put their own portal effect in it. `source/` is untouched. On the derived `SM_Rift` only,
7,238 inner faces (area 0.767 m² in pre-scale units) were deleted in Blender 4.5.3:

- After import the ring sat at the rear (+Y), axis along Y, plane XZ, centre ≈
  (-0.0037, 0.5867, 0.1423).
- Faces whose centroids fell inside a cylinder of radius 0.245 m about that centre,
  in a Y window covering the ring and its rear bulge, were removed if they were clearly inside
  the hole (radius < 0.20 m) or were magenta / emissive / axis-aligned (the membrane). The
  ring frame was kept.
- Triangle count went from 50,000 to 42,762 (still inside the 40–60k budget). The cut
  opens a hole through the ring; the inner rim is a raw Rodin/boolean-style edge and may want
  a cleanup pass on import.

Before / after renders: `portal_cut_before_after.png` (front, rear, 3/4, side, top).

## Normal convention

`source/texture_normal.png` (and the normal embedded in both the raw and the derived GLB) is
**OpenGL** (Y+): it is the glTF normal, B-channel is nearly 255 (outward Z), and the glTF 2.0 spec
requires OpenGL tangent-space normals. That matches how Rodin ships a glTF pack.

For Unreal, which expects **DirectX** (Y−), the delivery map `T_Rift_N.png` has the green
channel inverted (`G' = 1 - G`). Red and blue are unchanged. This is the same convention used for
the accepted Drone mesh_r1 and the R-027 terrain sets. When importing the loose maps, set N as a
Normalmap (DirectX) and do **not** also use the GLB-embedded OpenGL normal.

## Lead-note compliance (honest)

The Lead's concept-gate notes for 3D: white must stay the main hull colour (squad colour ~30%);
the portal ring must stay an open ring; engines need clean round nozzles; thin parts (wing tips,
gun barrels) thickened enough to survive at 40–60k tris.

- **White / magenta split.** After the portal-disc removal, area-weighted albedo (visible faces)
  is about **21.1% white+off-white**, **22.9% magenta**,
  36.8% dark mechanical, plus mid-grey. Before the cut, magenta was 27.3%
  (the filled disc was a large magenta surface). The fuselage reads white; the crescent wings are
  strongly magenta, in line with the approved r2 concept. Magenta is in the accent range and is
  not "mostly pink", but it is a heavy accent (the wings are almost fully magenta). White is the
  main fuselage colour, not a minority after the disc came out.
- **Open portal ring.** The raw model had a filled magenta disc (confirmed in the Rodin viewer
  and in the before renders). The derived `SM_Rift` has that inner surface removed; you can see
  through the ring. See `portal_cut_before_after.png`.
- **Engines.** Two fairly clean round rear nozzles sit under the portal ring (plus a central
  rear intake). Round enough to socket exhaust effects; some Rodin surface noise on the lips.
- **Thin parts.** Wing tips and the two nose guns have volume, not paper edges. Topology is
  Rodin's auto-triangulation, not clean quads.
- **Proportions.** Scaled so length (X) is exactly 500 cm. Resulting size is
  **500.0 × 417.4 × 226.0 cm (L×W×H)** vs the brief
  500 × 494 × 119. Width is -76.7 cm (-15.5%).
  Height is +107.0 cm (+89.9%) — the vertical portal ring makes the
  model substantially taller than the 119 cm brief, which is faithful to the approved concept.
  Uniform scale was not changed to force the brief height.
