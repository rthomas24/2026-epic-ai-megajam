## What was done
1. **Weld** `remove_doubles` 0.03 cm (verts 62,032 -> 21,617; max vertex move 0.027 cm). Loop UVs
   are per-corner so seams stay split. Degenerate slivers collapsed by the weld (~440 tris) went away.
2. **Debris**: after the weld the mesh had 83 islands: the main hull, the ragged ring remnant
   (2,678 tris) and 81 floating fragments (1-28 tris, mostly the old portal-cut leftovers around
   x = -130..-176 cm, plus one 4-tri chip at (204, 186, -105) cm and a 0.2 cm sliver). All removed
   (2,872 tris). Wingtips, nose guns and antennas are part of the main shell and were not touched
   (checked in the front/top/hero renders).
3. **Cut** (by face centroid, cm): rear block `x < -178, |y| < 102, -97 < z < 2`; ragged saddle
   above the deck `x < -178, |y| < 55, z >= 2`; old open dorsal cavity `-178 <= x < -114, |y| < 50, z > -22`
   (5,886 tris). The twin magenta fins (|y| 70-110, z 0-80) are outside the cut and kept whole.
4. **New hard-surface parts** (all closed solids, 0 open edges, built in `source/parts.py`):
   - **Portal ring**: revolve, 112 segments, centre exactly **(-170, 0, 55) cm = `SOCKET_Portal`**,
     axis +X. White ceramic outer collar R 58.0 cm (top lands on the original Z max 112.996 cm),
     chamfers, charcoal side faces with an inset emissive bevel accent each side, charcoal
     tubular inner rim R 47 cm with a **recessed emissive channel** (6 cm wide, 2.6 cm deep).
     **Clear opening 94 cm** (r1 ~ 95). Six magenta armour clamps, a gunmetal yoke cradle under
     the ring, seated into a dorsal spine fairing and a deck plate. Y-symmetric.
   - **Rear**: three bevelled nacelles (outer at (y +-64, z -34), centre at (0, -34)) with
     gunmetal/charcoal bodies, white armour bands and magenta trim rings, a charcoal keel, a belly
     skirt, and the deck. **Outer nozzles** (`Engine_0/1`): chamfered collar, 24 petal facets on
     the bell, lip at x = -250.0 cm, dark interior wall, dim-emissive inner ring, **sealed turbine
     back plate**, 12 twisted blades and a bright magenta emissive core cone. **Centre nacelle**
     (`Engine`): closed recessed intake with a sealed diffuser plate, 10 stator vanes, emissive
     cone and dim-emissive ring. Nothing at the back is hollow now: every engine interior ends in
     a solid plate.
   - **Zero gaps**: all **518 open-edge vertices** the cut left on the Rodin shell lie inside the
     new solids (ray-parity test, `source/cover.py`: 0 outside). The remaining 10 open edges on the
     body are pre-existing pinholes in Rodin's mesh away from the rear.
5. **Normals**: kept hull: custom normals cleared, smooth by angle 40 deg + WeightedNormal
   (face area, keep sharp). New parts: smooth by angle 32 deg, sharp at material-zone borders,
   WeightedNormal.
6. **UVs**: kept faces keep their r1 UVs **exactly (max delta 0.0 over all 33,550 faces, tracked
   by original face index)**. New parts: smart UV (seams at zone borders so every shell is one
   material zone), then packed by `source/pack.py` into the free cells of an occupancy mask of all
   kept UV triangles (dilated 3 px @1K). See `verify_uv.txt`: **0 overlapping pixels at 2048 and
   4096**, min gap new->kept 11 px @2K, nothing outside 0-1. New-part texel density 1.18 px/cm
   (kept 2.58 px/cm); new parts are flat-colour per zone, so this costs no visible detail.
7. **Textures** (`source/tex_rift.py`, `texlib.py`), kept-island pixels only plus new zones:
   - BC: two bilateral passes, panel-line high-pass re-injected, chroma of white ceramic and magenta
     paint pulled toward a smoothed local value. Local luminance std on paint 2.71 -> 1.71.
   - ORM: R = 255 (no AO, as r1), G roughness median + bilateral (local noise 2.0 -> 1.0), B metallic
     median + smoothstep 0.25-0.75 for a clean metal/non-metal split.
   - N (DirectX, Y-): low band (sigma 3 px) scaled 0.45 to soften wing triangle-soup lumps, high band
     (panel lines) kept, light bilateral; renormalised.
   - E: only bright, saturated magenta/violet is kept (r1's E was a dim copy of the whole BC):
     grey/muddy lit pixels 264,060 -> 1. Kept emissive saturation floor 0.62, +25 %, light unsharp.
   - New zones: white ceramic, charcoal metal, gunmetal, magenta paint, magenta emissive, dark
     interior, dim emissive; flat normal (128,128,255). A Cycles AO bake of the final mesh
     (48 spp, denoised) darkens **new-part pixels only**; kept and new UV areas are disjoint
     (0 px overlap), so kept Rodin BC/ORM pixels get no AO.
   - Unused atlas space is filled with the nearest used pixel (mip padding); E is zero outside a
     8 px gutter.
8. **Export**: FBX `apply_unit_scale`, `FBX_SCALE_UNITS`, forward -Z, up Y, face smoothing,
   tangents, triangulated: **UnitScaleFactor = OriginalUnitScaleFactor = 100**. Contains
   `SOCKET_{Muzzle_0,Muzzle_1,Engine,Engine_0,Engine_1,Portal,Cockpit,WingTip_L,WingTip_R}` empties
   at the same positions as `ArtSource/EngShips/Rift/SM_Rift.blend` (UE: Muzzle +-37/-60 at 252,
   Engine -252/0/-34, Engine_0/1 -252/-+64/-34, Portal -170/0/55, Cockpit 60/0/0, WingTips 238/-+176/-109).
   GLB: centimetre values, `export_yup=false` (Z up, X forward, same as r1), embeds the OpenGL normal.
   No UCX collision in this drop (the EngShips pipeline builds it).

## Heat mask
`heatmask_r1/T_Rift_HeatMask.png` still lines up with every kept face (UVs unchanged). Its 32 px
dilation spilled into the freed atlas space the new parts now use (136k px), so
**`T_Rift_HeatMask_r2.png`** is supplied: byte-identical to heatmask_r1 on all kept pixels, zeroed
under the new rear parts (trailing surfaces stay cool on entry). Use it with mesh_r2.

## Import (Unreal)
FBX uniform scale 1.0, Import Normals (keep custom normals), combine meshes, Nanite OK.
BC sRGB; N Normalmap, already DirectX, do not flip; ORM Masks sRGB off (R AO, G rough, B metal);
E sRGB, emissive. Single material slot `M_Rift`.
