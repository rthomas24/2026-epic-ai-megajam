## Prompts
### Three-direction sheet prompt (seeds 1101 and 2202, verbatim)
No input image.

```
Original production-quality AAA sci-fi concept art: a model-sheet of three different one-handed energy-blade HILTS for a starfighter pilot, all in the same squad material language. Show only the physical weapon hardware: hilt, guard and emitter. Do not draw a solid cylindrical glowing sword blade; this must not look like a lightsaber. Materials: white ceramic-composite plates over dark gunmetal internals, thin cyan (#3FE9FF) accent strips and a few small emissive pips, no logos, no insignia, no letters, no numbers, no watermarks. Each weapon is 30 to 45 cm long, chunky enough to read from a third-person chase camera, clearly one-handed. Place the three designs in one row, left to right, on a flat plain mid-grey studio background, soft even lighting, no scenery.

LEFT design: an open-frame fork emitter with a deflector guard. A short ribbed gunmetal grip, a swept forward deflector guard like a small buckler or crossguard plate in white ceramic with a cyan edge, and a split open fork / wishbone emitter head in gunmetal with white ceramic sleeves. The two tines leave an empty open gap where an energy edge would later form; the gap stays empty or shows only a very faint translucent cyan ghost, never a solid glowing stick.

CENTER design: a folding single-edged sabre. A white ceramic grip with a dark rubberized wrap and a folding knuckle spine. In this view it is UNFOLDED: a slim single-edged emitter spine has swung out and locked, with a short cutting-side rail and a cyan strip along the spine. It reads as a folding tool or short sabre hardware, not a tube. A smaller companion object beside it may show the same weapon FOLDED, compact against the grip, emitter spine stowed.

RIGHT design: a compact survival blade. Shorter and thicker than the others, a stout utility hilt with a visible cylindrical fuel cell in the pommel that glows soft cyan, a protective emitter cage, and a short wedge-shaped emitter. Rugged, field-repairable, no elegant dueling look.

Crisp hard-surface product design, clean bevels and panel gaps, hero three-quarter views of all three, identical lighting. Original designs that do not resemble any existing game, film or brand weapon.
```
