Points for Nick's decision

Independent review finds this ready for the same refined-foundation decision as Lancer. Known limitations: the portal becomes a thin bar from the side; its supports are simpler than the flowing hull; broad violet paint reads bright lavender and uniformly fresh; rear and underside structure are sparser than Lancer. These remain visible rather than being treated as final AAA polish.
