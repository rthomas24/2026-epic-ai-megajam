# Audio Prototype V1
Nick authorizes existing owned/free source implementation (2026-11-12); subjective approval is prototype-only.
Play events: throttle, acceleration, primary fire, missile, explosion, hull/shield impacts, three switches, target/ability feedback and two music states.
Feedback must start promptly, stop with clean fades, survive repeated playback, and remain adjustable by SFX/UI/music group.
Sources: selected recordings/designs from 50 Free Game Sounds Pack, Hove Terminal UI LITE and Marika Schanz Atmospheric Worlds; no new acquisition or synthesized generator.
Use the skill4096f47 category levels; the anchor is primary fire at -6dB playback. Engines are a recorded computer machinery substitution, not a final spacecraft turbine.
Native demonstration only; no shared gameplay/config/maps/data assets or main integration. Missing purpose-recorded engine/boost, bespoke missile/shield/ability identity and alternate source takes remain review items.
