# Sonniss GameAudioGDC 2024 — supplied parts 1 and 2

Nicholas supplied these two archives on November 13, 2026 and requested that Ryan and the team receive this large pool of potentially useful sound effects. These files are candidates for audition, not an approved replacement set.

## Contents

304 original WAV files plus the publisher documents, extracted without audio conversion or renaming from parts 1 and 2 of the nine-part 2024 bundle. The archive layout is preserved under Bundle01 and Bundle02. Total extracted size is approximately 6.71 GiB. Parts 3–9 are not included.

catalog.csv lists each exact path, byte size and SHA-256. archives.json records the original archive names, sizes and hashes. The Downloads paths disappeared during intake; the archive payloads were located in the Recycle Bin and read without changing or deleting those originals.

## Use in Triad Drift

Browse the original library folders and catalog before downloading the whole set. WAV files are in Git LFS. Audition and select source material first; import only selected effects into the existing Unreal audio workflow. This ArtSource library is outside Content and is not intended to be packaged wholesale. Preserve existing sources and cue routing. Nicholas retains listening approval.

For Lancer, assess sci-fi energy body and tail layers, physical mechanisms, distinct shield versus hull hits, and listen at the actual rapid-fire rate. For ambience, look for machinery, electrical textures, environmental and room recordings. The bundle also contains unrelated material; no claim is made that every file fits this game or the quality target.
