# Verdance ground prop kit (engineering-made head start)

Ten small static meshes that dress the ground the player walks on around the Verdance landing pad:
three giant glowing mushrooms, two ferns, two small glowing plants and three mossy rocks. Verdance
is the bioluminescent jungle planet (features.md ART-06): rounded, flowing shapes in deep teal and
violet, with cyan, magenta and lime glow.

These are **engineering-made head-start props**, built at the human lead's request
(`MESSAGEBOARD.md` R-015). The art director (Nick) reviews them afterwards and may restyle or
replace any piece. The brief is in [`brief.md`](brief.md).

**Stage reached (AAA.md): technically verified.** Not presentation approved (that is the art
director's call), not integrated (nothing has been imported into Unreal), not gameplay validated
(nobody has seen them in the running game).

## Texture sets

Three shared atlases, each 1024 x 1024, four maps per set. One material slot per mesh.

| Set | Used by | Maps |
|---|---|---|
| `T_Verd_Mushrooms_*` | Mushroom_01, _02, _03 | `_BC` base colour (sRGB), `_N` normal (DirectX, do not flip green), `_ORM` AO / roughness / metallic (linear), `_E` emissive colour (sRGB) |
| `T_Verd_Flora_*` | Fern_01, _02, GlowPlant_01, _02 | same four |
| `T_Verd_Rocks_*` | Rock_01, _02, _03 | same four |

- Emissive is its own map. Multiply it by **emission strength 2.0** in the Unreal material (the value
  is in every manifest under `texture_set.emission_strength`). The map carries the brightness, so
  bulbs and gill ridges are dimmer than rims and spots.
- Metallic is 0 everywhere.
- A little baked ambient occlusion (30 %) is multiplied into the base colour for cavity shading.

## How it was made

Everything is generated by code in Blender 5.2.2, headless. There is no hand modelling, no AI mesh
generator and no third-party asset in this kit.

- **Geometry** (`scripts/build_verdance.py`): each asset is assembled from a few closed shells made
  by `propkit.Builder`:
  - `lathe` for caps, bulbs, pods and mounds
  - `sweep` / `tube` / `blade` for stems, stalks, spirals and fronds (fronds are real folded blades,
    not alpha cards, so no masked material is needed)
  - `rock` for the boulders: a lumpy sphere sliced by planes, with the edges chamfered
- **Look**: every primitive carries its colour, roughness and glow as corner colour attributes. One
  procedural material per texture set reads them and adds noise, bump, moss and lichen.
- **Shared atlas**: the assets of a set are joined into one temporary mesh, unwrapped with the
  repo's `smart_uv` helper and packed into one 0-1 square, then baked once.
- **Bake**: `.claude/skills/blender-texturing/scripts/bake_texture_set.py` (Cycles, CPU, 8 samples,
  24 for ambient occlusion).
- **Checks**: `.claude/skills/blender-mcp/scripts/asset_report.py` and `assembly_check.py`.
- **Review renders**: `.claude/skills/blender-mcp/scripts/review_renders.py`, plus the kit sheets
  made by `propkit.py`.
- **Export**: `.claude/skills/blender-unreal-export/scripts/export_for_unreal.py`, one FBX per asset.

### Regenerate

From the repository root (seven to eight minutes, most of it UV packing):

```
"C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe" --background --factory-startup --python ArtSource/EngProps/Verdance/scripts/build_verdance.py
```

That one command rebuilds everything in this folder: geometry, textures, collision, checks, review
renders, the `.blend`, `.fbx` and manifest of every asset, and the FBX round trip. For faster
iteration:

```
... --python ArtSource/EngProps/Verdance/scripts/build_verdance.py -- --stage clay                 # shapes only, a few seconds
... --python ArtSource/EngProps/Verdance/scripts/build_verdance.py -- --stage review --fast        # textures + review renders, loose packing
... --python ArtSource/EngProps/Verdance/scripts/build_verdance.py -- --stage clay --only Fern     # some assets only
```

`--fast` and `--only` write looser or partial atlases into `textures/`; always finish with the full
command above before committing.

## What was verified

All of it by script, on Blender 5.2.2 LTS, headless. Results are in `review/Verdance_summary.json`
and one `review/<AssetName>_report.json` per asset.

| Check | Tool | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Mesh health: closed shells, no flipped or inside-out faces, no duplicate or loose vertices, no zero-area faces, scale 1, one UV map, one material slot | `asset_report.py` | 10 of 10 assets with 0 errors and 0 warnings (render mesh and collision hull) |
| No floating parts: every shell intersects the main body | `assembly_check.py` | 10 of 10 pass |
| Triangle budget | counted on the exported mesh | 10 of 10 inside their budget |
| Pivot at the base centre | footprint centre against the origin | largest offset 13 cm (see the note below) |
| Shared atlas: UVs inside 0-1, and the atlas UVs copied back onto the right vertices | `propkit.atlas_bake` | pass for all three sets (the build stops otherwise) |
| FBX round trip: each FBX re-imported into an empty Blender file; exact names (`SM_...`, `UCX_SM_..._00`), triangle count equals the manifest, all triangles, one UV map, one material slot, scale 1, height within 0.6 cm | `propkit.fbx_round_trip` | 10 of 10 pass |
| Collision: exactly one hull where the brief asks for one, none elsewhere | manifest and round trip | 5 hulls, as listed in the table |

Pivot note: the footprint centre is measured from everything within 8 cm of the ground. On
`GlowPlant_01` that includes drooping leaf tips (13 cm off) and on `Mushroom_03` the buttress lobes
(9 cm off); the pivot itself is at the root of the plant and the centre of the trunk. Every other
piece is within 3 cm.

Git LFS: every `.blend`, `.fbx` and `.png` in this folder is stored through LFS (checked with
`git lfs ls-files` after staging).

## What was not verified

- **Nothing has been imported into Unreal.** Scale, pivot, collision pairing, normal-map direction
  and the look under Lumen and bloom are unchecked in the engine. The FBX round trip was done in
  Blender only.
- **No Unreal material exists for a shared atlas yet.** The repo's `ue_import_assets.py` looks for
  textures named after each mesh (`T_<Asset>_*`), so it will import these meshes but will not find
  their textures. Each manifest names its set under `texture_set`; one material instance per set
  (three for the kit) is all that is needed.
- **The glow has not been seen with bloom.** In Blender the emissive values stay below white-out;
  with Unreal's bloom they will read stronger. Lower `EmissiveStrength` on the material instance
  rather than editing the maps.
- **Texture density is low on the big pieces** (about 95 px/m on Mushroom_02 and _03), the price of
  three 1024 atlases for ten assets. It holds up for soft organic surfaces; it will look soft if the
  player presses their face against the 4.5 m mushroom.
- **Not played.** No one has walked around these in first person in the game.

## Fresh-eyes review

The skill's fresh-eyes review was run twice, each time by a new agent that had not seen the build,
given only the brief, the rubric (`blender-game-asset/references/aaa-quality-bar.md`), the review
renders and the numbers.

| Round | Silhouette | Proportion | Detail | Construction | Edges | Materials | Style fit | Technical |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 5 |
| 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 5 |

- **After round 1** these were changed: more segments on the cap rims; gill glow restricted to the
  ridges with a dark membrane between them; fronds rebuilt as smooth folded blades; round fiddlehead
  stalks; the dark plinths under the small plants shrunk to a buried root flare; moss painted across
  the facets instead of along their edges; lichen in patches of varied size; lures hung from
  Mushroom_03's rims where the player looks.
- **After round 2** these were changed: gill lines narrowed, faded toward the stem and kept dimmer
  than the rim; Fern_01 given more arch, scalloped edges and glow on the tips only; rocks made
  rounder with wider chamfers and the tooth of Rock_03 slumped into a dome; bulbs rounder with a
  lower peak and soft ribs. **These round 2 changes have not been re-scored.**
- **The score did not move between rounds, so the review loop was stopped there**, as the skill's
  stop rule says (two rounds without improvement: stop and hand it to a human).

**Honest reading: the kit is "decent indie" (3), not the skill's ship threshold (4).** The weakest
point both reviewers named is visible faceting on organic forms (cap rims, bulbs, trunk flare) at
these triangle budgets, and code-built plants in general: the skill itself routes flora to an
image-to-3D or licensed-asset workflow for that reason. The art director should expect to restyle or
replace the ferns, glow plants and Rock_01 first; the three mushrooms were judged the strongest
pieces.

- **Exporter quirk found:** `export_for_unreal.py` fails in its own clean-up
  (`'NoneType' object has no attribute 'select_set'`, line 316) when the asset root has no child
  object and no collision is exported. The FBX and manifest are already written by then.
  `propkit.package()` avoids it with a temporary empty child that is not exported and not saved.

  (not an absolute path on this machine), `textures` lists the shared set's files, and
