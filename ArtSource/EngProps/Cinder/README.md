# Cinder ground prop kit (engineering-made head start)

Nine small static meshes that dress the ground the player walks on around the Cinder landing pad:
four crystal clusters (two cyan, two amber), three basalt rocks and two groups of hexagonal basalt
columns. Cinder is the volcanic planet (features.md ART-06): vertical, crystalline, sharp shapes in
black basalt, with cyan and amber crystal glow and a little magma. Everything is flat shaded on
purpose.

These are **engineering-made head-start props**, built at the human lead's request
(`MESSAGEBOARD.md` R-015). The art director (Nick) reviews them afterwards and may restyle or
replace any piece. The brief is in [`brief.md`](brief.md).

**Stage reached (AAA.md): technically verified.** Not presentation approved (that is the art
director's call), not integrated (nothing has been imported into Unreal), not gameplay validated
(nobody has seen them in the running game).

## Texture sets

Three shared atlases, each 1024 x 1024, four maps per set. One material slot per mesh.

| Set | Used by | Maps |
|---|---|---|
| `T_Cind_Crystals_*` | Crystal_01, _02, _03, _04 (cyan and amber share the set) | `_BC` base colour (sRGB), `_N` normal (DirectX, do not flip green), `_ORM` AO / roughness / metallic (linear), `_E` emissive colour (sRGB) |
| `T_Cind_Basalt_*` | Basalt_01, _02, _03 | same four |
| `T_Cind_Columns_*` | BasaltColumn_01, _02 | same four |

- Emissive is its own map. Multiply it by **emission strength 1.5** in the Unreal material (the value
  is in every manifest under `texture_set.emission_strength`).
- Crystals: every facet has its own brightness (the narrow edge facets are the brightest lines), and
  the glow runs hot at the root and dim at the point, so the facets read in a dark scene. The rock
  the crystals grow from carries a little of their light.
- Metallic is 0 everywhere.
- A little baked ambient occlusion (30 %) is multiplied into the base colour for cavity shading.

## How it was made

Everything is generated by code in Blender 5.2.2, headless. There is no hand modelling, no AI mesh
generator and no third-party asset in this kit.

- **Geometry** (`scripts/build_cinder.py`), all from `propkit.Builder`:
  - crystals: `sweep` of an irregular prism whose wide faces alternate with narrow edge facets,
    with growth steps cut into the shaft and an off-centre or chisel point
  - basalt rocks and the rock under the crystals: `rock`, a lumpy sphere sliced by many planes and
    chamfered; `Basalt_01` is one block cut in two by a shared plane, so the halves match
  - columns: hexagons on a jittered grid (neighbours share corners), each grown 1.5 cm past its
    outline so the shells interlock, with a chamfered joint groove and a slanted, chipped cap
  - secondary pieces are slid against the main one until they intersect, so nothing floats
- **Look**: every primitive carries its colour, roughness and glow as corner colour attributes. One
  procedural material per texture set reads them and adds noise, bump, ash and ember cracks.
- **Shared atlas**: the assets of a set are joined into one temporary mesh, unwrapped with the
  repo's `smart_uv` helper and packed into one 0-1 square, then baked once.
- **Bake**: `.claude/skills/blender-texturing/scripts/bake_texture_set.py` (Cycles, CPU, 8 samples,
  24 for ambient occlusion).
- **Checks**: `.claude/skills/blender-mcp/scripts/asset_report.py` and `assembly_check.py`.
- **Review renders**: `.claude/skills/blender-mcp/scripts/review_renders.py`, plus the kit sheets
  made by `propkit.py`.
- **Export**: `.claude/skills/blender-unreal-export/scripts/export_for_unreal.py`, one FBX per asset.

### Regenerate

From the repository root (about five minutes, most of it UV packing):

```
"C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 5.2\blender.exe" --background --factory-startup --python ArtSource/EngProps/Cinder/scripts/build_cinder.py
```

That one command rebuilds everything in this folder: geometry, textures, collision, checks, review
renders, the `.blend`, `.fbx` and manifest of every asset, and the FBX round trip. For faster
iteration:

```
... --python ArtSource/EngProps/Cinder/scripts/build_cinder.py -- --stage clay                 # shapes only, a few seconds
... --python ArtSource/EngProps/Cinder/scripts/build_cinder.py -- --stage review --fast        # textures + review renders, loose packing
... --python ArtSource/EngProps/Cinder/scripts/build_cinder.py -- --stage clay --only Crystal     # some assets only
```

`--fast` and `--only` write looser or partial atlases into `textures/`; always finish with the full
command above before committing.

and one `review/<AssetName>_report.json` per asset.

Git LFS: every `.blend`, `.fbx` and `.png` in this folder is stored through LFS (checked with
`git lfs ls-files` after staging).

## What was not verified

- **Nothing has been imported into Unreal.** Scale, pivot, collision pairing, normal-map direction
  and the look under Lumen and bloom are unchecked in the engine. The FBX round trip was done in
  Blender only.
- **No Unreal material exists for a shared atlas yet.** The repo's `ue_import_assets.py` looks for
  textures named after each mesh (`T_<Asset>_*`), so it will import these meshes but will not find
  their textures. Each manifest names its set under `texture_set`; one material instance per set
  (three for the kit) is all that is needed.
- **The glow has not been seen with bloom.** In Blender the emissive values stay below white-out;
  with Unreal's bloom they will read stronger. Lower `EmissiveStrength` on the material instance
  rather than editing the maps.
- **Texture density is low on the big pieces** (about 60 px/m on the two column groups and 90 px/m on the
  tall crystals), the price of three 1024 atlases for nine assets. Flat-shaded facets hide it at
  walking distance; the column sides will look soft up close.
- **Not played.** No one has walked around these in first person in the game.

## Fresh-eyes review

The skill's fresh-eyes review was run twice, each time by a new agent that had not seen the build,
given only the brief, the rubric (`blender-game-asset/references/aaa-quality-bar.md`), the review
renders and the numbers.

| Round | Silhouette | Proportion | Detail | Construction | Edges | Materials | Style fit | Technical |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 |
| 2 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 | 4 |

- **After round 1** these were changed: Crystal_02 rebuilt as flat blades with oblique chisel tips
  (it was a bundle of pencils); Crystal_04 given a chisel tip and dimmer buttresses; a stronger
  root-to-tip glow falloff; Basalt_01 rebuilt as one block split in two with matching faces;
  rounded pebbles replaced by angular shards; column caps slanted with a chipped corner and each
  side face given its own tone; ember cracks made fainter.
- **After round 2** these were changed: the pale crystal tips made deeper and more saturated, the
  crystal surface made less glossy (no white facets or sparkle), the glow falloff made steeper; the
  ember cracks of Basalt_03 cut again and confined to the lowest 35 cm; the loose-looking chips
  removed from the columns and the splinter from Basalt_01. **These round 2 changes have not been
  re-scored.**
- **The score did not move between rounds, so the review loop was stopped there**, as the skill's
  stop rule says (two rounds without improvement: stop and hand it to a human).

**Honest reading: the kit is "decent indie" (3, style fit 4), not the skill's ship threshold (4).**
Both reviewers found the crystals and the taller column group the strongest pieces and the small
basalt rocks (Basalt_01, Basalt_02) the weakest: dark, low-contrast lumps that disappear against a
dark ground. Both confirmed that the crystals glow in the dark with every facet still readable, and
that Cinder and Verdance are unmistakably different in colour and shape.

- **Exporter quirk found:** `export_for_unreal.py` fails in its own clean-up
  (`'NoneType' object has no attribute 'select_set'`, line 316) when the asset root has no child
  object and no collision is exported. The FBX and manifest are already written by then.
  `propkit.package()` avoids it with a temporary empty child that is not exported and not saved.

  (not an absolute path on this machine), `textures` lists the shared set's files, and
  `texture_set` and `collision_note` are added.
