The pack is a 4K leaf atlas (ferns, monstera, oak, split and long tropical leaves) with opacity, no meshes. `../scripts/prep_fab_plants.py` made a 1024 atlas (`T_Fab_VerdancePlants_BC` RGBA with the opacity in alpha, greens to Verdance teal and the red-brown leaves to violet; `_N`, OpenGL flipped) and built three card clumps from bent quads on nine hand-measured leaf islands (the automatic island search merged neighbours): `SM_Fab_VerdancePlant_Fern` (7 cards, 28 tris, about 95 x 46 cm), `_Broad` (6, 24 tris, 111 x 47 cm), `_Long` (8, 32 tris, 102 x 85 cm). New master `M_TD_FabFoliageCard` (masked, two-sided, Tint, SelfIllum glow, instanced-mesh use), instances `MI_Fab_VerdancePlants_Teal` (on the meshes) and `_Violet` (tint swap). No collision, no Nanite. `/Game/TriadDrift/Gameplay/Props/Fab/VerdancePlants/`. Sheet `review/Fab_VerdancePlants_material_sheet.png` (the three clumps overlapped at the origin). Est. cooked 2.7 MB. Use: Verdance near-ground cover (#194), dense clusters at scale 0.7-1.6, random yaw. Not yet seen in the engine: the master compiles on first load.
