# SM_Fab_PowerCore (Fab "Radiation Core Power Trash For Cyberpunk Map")

## What it is

A depleted, battered radiation power cell: black armoured casing with chipped silver edges and orange rust, a glowing cyan
radiation symbol, a cracked "0%" display with "POWER LEVEL" text, handles, warning stickers and loose blue and orange cables
at one end. One small 4-tri sliver floats 1.9 cm off the body (cable tip); harmless at prop distance, left as is.

## How it was made (Ryan's side, Claude Code, Nov 14 2026)

1. Archive downloaded with `gh release download asset-sources-nicholas-20261114` (Ryan's approval) to
   `Builds/intake/PowerCore/` (git-ignored, not committed); ZIP SHA-256 matched the issue; the archive's own
   `SHA256SUMS.txt` matched every file. Treated as untrusted: `.blend` opened only with `--disable-autoexec`.
2. `scripts/prep_fab.py` (Blender 5.2.2 headless): one mesh `SM_Fab_PowerCore`, transforms applied, origin on the floor centre,
   front kept on -Y; textures re-written at 2048 as `T_Fab_PowerCore_BC/N/ORM, _E` (N DirectX; ORM = AO, roughness, metallic).
   Report: `prep_report.json`.
3. `export_for_unreal.py` (blender-unreal-export skill) wrote `export/SM_Fab_PowerCore.fbx` with one auto convex hull
   `UCX_SM_Fab_PowerCore_00` and the manifest. (The skill script's cleanup loop raised on a stale `None` in the view layer after a
   successful export; run with a local copy guarded by `vl.update()` and `if o is not None`. Reported to the Lead.)
4. `scripts/ue_import_fab.py` (UnrealEditor-Cmd, -nullrhi, via `Tools/td-python.ps1`): Interchange with
   `PL_Ship_StaticMesh`, Nanite on, MI on `M_TD_PropTextured`. Report: `../ue_import_report.json`.
5. Checks: `review/asset_report.json`, `review/assembly_check.json`, sheets `review/Fab_PowerCore_material_sheet.png`, `_clay_sheet.png`.
