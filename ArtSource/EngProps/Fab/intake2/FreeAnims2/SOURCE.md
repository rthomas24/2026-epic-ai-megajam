## What is in it

Native Unreal content (manifest lists UE 5.6-5.8): 12 clips on the UE5 mannequin skeleton (BenchConversation3, Crucifixion, DrinkingWineFromGlass, Eats, FightingIdle, GraspLying, Hack, LevitatingFireballCharge2, Row, TakeDown, TakesOutGun, WalkingVideoCall), the Epic Manny/Quinn demo meshes and textures (with the UE logo) and an overview map. 99 MB raw.

## Licence (as stated in the package and its listing evidence)

Fab Standard; NoAI / not AI-generated. The Epic mannequin content stays under the Unreal Engine EULA. No standalone redistribution.

## Verdict

PARTS ONLY. Four clips are useful: Hack (console and terminal interaction for the pilot), FightingIdle (crew alert pose), TakeDown (a finisher or crew grab), TakesOutGun (trooper draw). They retarget to our pilot and crew the same way as the template clips. Skip the rest (Crucifixion is unsuitable). About 1-2 h for the four, with captures.
