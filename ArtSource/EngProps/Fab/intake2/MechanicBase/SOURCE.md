## What is in it

Native Unreal content (UE 5.6 pack; Nick's side loaded all 209 packages in 5.9 with no errors): 120 static meshes (modular floors, walls, door frames, consoles, lamps, pipes, an elevator, robot-arm parts, a mech torso), 12 Blueprints (doors, elevator, lamps, spline), 10 materials and 30 instances, 30 textures (five trim sheets at 8K), one Niagara system, two demo maps; one FBX (SM_FLOOR_01). SM_LANDSCAPE_01 is 524,288 tris; every mesh has one LOD; SM_FLOOR_01 and SM_GLASS_01 have no simple collision. About 705 MB raw. Per-mesh triangle counts not read (no Unreal load here).

## Licence (as stated in the package and its listing evidence)

Fab Standard; commercial use and modification allowed; publisher attribution optional; NoAI label. No standalone redistribution.

## Verdict

PARTS ONLY. Clean, original hard-surface sci-fi in grey-green with pale-blue lights: good dressing for the Dominion interior (consoles, door frames, lamps, conduits) and outposts, recoloured to the Null black and red. Not as walkable floors or walls (R-043: our kit stays the walkable surface). About 3-4 h for a curated set of 15-25 props: migrate, 8K to 2K, Null material instances, collision check.
