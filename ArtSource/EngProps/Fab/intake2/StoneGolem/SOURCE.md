## Verdict

SKIP. A crowned, spiked stone knight in dark-fantasy greys: it reads as a different game next to the Null Armada (black armour, red glow, white eyes), and our Warden already fills the mini-boss role. Generic design, no obvious borrowed IP. The clip set is good, but a re-skin plus retarget is 6 h or more.
