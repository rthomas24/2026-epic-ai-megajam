## What is in it

One prop in four meshes (11,280 tris): a 9 m square truss with diagonal bracing carrying bundled ribbed cables, 0.75-1.1 m across. Blend, FBX, OBJ/MTL and 3ds Max files; 4096 PNG maps for the tube set (BC, emissive, height, metallic, normal, roughness) and the cable set (BC, metallic, normal, roughness). The .blend also references texture sets B-G and a grid set that are not in the package. `SKY_002.exr` has no stated provenance and is not to be used.

## Licence (as stated in the package and its listing evidence)

Fab Standard (Personal / Professional offers, same rights; acquired tier not recorded). Seller marks NoAI. No standalone redistribution.

## Verdict

TAKE, as non-walkable dressing. Original, generic industrial design. Orange rusty truss as supplied; recolour to Null black with red running lights for the Dominion exterior and space set pieces, or keep the amber for the Cinder outpost gantries and dig site. Fills the gap of long structural girders between our kit pieces. About 2 h (join, 2K maps, recolour, import, collision).
