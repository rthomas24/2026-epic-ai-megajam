Not imported. Evaluation only (Lead's request, Nov 14 2026); no Content and no CREDITS change until the Lead picks.

- **Source:** https://www.fab.com/listings/dbbc47a2-633e-4963-8e9c-d2b005402ed8 (issue #183); supplied by Nicholas Tosti in the private release `asset-sources-nicholas-20261114`, downloaded with Ryan's approval.
- **Archive SHA-256:** `c07b2806cf08a92524115b56b0fd4ef03a52ae60cf3cefce78d33981d7556aa1` (matches the issue). The original stays in the git-ignored `Builds/intake2/DissolveURP/`; nothing from the archive was run.

## What is in it

A Unity .unitypackage: 3 ShaderLab shaders (hologram, slice, top-down dissolve), 3 C# scripts, 4 materials, a demo scene, a documentation PDF and a demo mannequin (motion-dummy_male, a Reallusion Character Creator export) with its five textures. No noise or mask textures. Read as a tar only; nothing installed or run.

## Licence (as stated in the package and its listing evidence)

Fab Standard; the listing marks it AI-generated. The demo character's upstream rights are not cleared.

## Verdict

SKIP. Nothing usable in Unreal: the shaders are Unity code and the only images belong to the demo character. If we want a dissolve, it is about 1 h as our own Unreal material (noise and edge glow), which also keeps it original.
