# Scatter kit (engineering-made)

Fourteen cheap static meshes for the planet scatter (`ATDTerrainScatter`, T4; `docs/eng/WORLD-DENSITY-PLAN.md` 3.1 and
4.2): Verdance ground cover and canopy clumps, boulders for both planets, charred trees for Cinder. The brief is in
[`brief.md`](brief.md).

**Stage reached (AAA.md): technically verified.** Built, imported, and seen in the running game on Verdance (on foot
at about 5 m, and from 300 m altitude and 800 m away, beside the existing trees). Not presentation approved, not yet
used by the scatter, not seen on Cinder, no frame-rate measurement with thousands of instances.

Everything is generated by code in Blender 5.2.2, headless. No AI mesh generator, no third-party asset.

## Materials

| Asset | What it is |
|---|---|
| `T_Scatter_Atlas_BC`, `T_Scatter_Atlas_E` | The only textures: 512 px, no mips. Left quarter: 8 colour ramps (root to tip). Right: tiling charred bark with ember cracks |
| `M_TD_ScatterFoliage` | Two-sided, lit. Parameters: `Tint`, `EmissiveStrength`, `SelfIllum`, `Roughness`, `Specular` |
| `M_TD_ScatterCanopy` | The same, masked: dithers **out** nearer than `NearFadeStart` (700 m) + `NearFadeLength` (100 m); mottling and glints from `T_Terrain_Detail_M` projected down in world space (`MottleScale`, `MottleDark`, `MottleBright`, `SparkleStrength`) |
| `M_TD_ScatterRock` | Boulders: the terrain's `T_Terrain_Rock_M` / `_N` on the mesh UVs (5 m per tile). Parameters: `Tint`, `TopTint` (upward faces), `TopAmount`, `TopStart`, `TopSharpness`, `UVScale` |
| `MI_Scatter_Foliage`, `MI_Scatter_Char` | Ground cover; charred trees |
| `MI_Scatter_Canopy`, `MI_Scatter_Canopy_NoFade` | Far-ring clumps (fade near); the same without the fade |
| `MI_Scatter_Rock_Verdance`, `MI_Scatter_Rock_Cinder` | Grey-teal rock with moss on top; black basalt with ash on top. **The boulder meshes default to the Verdance instance: on Cinder set the Cinder instance on the instanced component** |

## Rebuild

```
blender --background --factory-startup --python ArtSource/EngProps/Scatter/scripts/build_scatter.py
powershell -File Tools\td-python.ps1 -Script ArtSource\EngProps\Scatter\scripts\ue_import_scatter.py
```

The first writes every `.blend`, `.fbx`, manifest, the atlas and the review sheets; the second imports them and builds
the materials (look values are in `INSTANCES` at the top of the import script). A master material that already exists
is kept: delete its `.uasset` to rebuild its graph.

## Seen in the game

Captures in `review/ingame/` (Verdance, the real `-game` build, test actors spawned with `TD.Dev.SpawnClass` from
temporary Blueprints that were deleted afterwards; no map or existing asset was saved).

## Known weak points

- Canopy clumps are faceted lumps: right at 800 m and beyond, wrong any closer (they read as coloured boulders at
  200 m). Use them in the far ring only, with the fading instance.
- Ground cover does not move in the wind, and is flat-shaded geometry, not leaf cards.
- The glowing pods of `GlowFlora_01` are close to white at their tips in the game's bloom.
- Ember cracks on the charred trees are an even net of lines; fine at 10 m, patterned at 2 m.
- `Canopy_03` is 28 triangles over the 800 budget.
